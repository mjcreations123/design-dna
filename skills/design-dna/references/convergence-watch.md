# Post-render convergence review

Use this only after a real candidate has been rendered and observed without
motif priming. It helps identify generic first-draft residue and unexplained
producer fingerprints. It does not detect AI authorship, assign an "AI
score," ban ingredients, or tell the next project to use the opposite style.

## Observe before loading the watch

Bind the exact build, routes, states, browser, widths, preferences, and capture
artifacts. Review the candidate first and record:

- what it is and what a visitor can understand or do;
- what attracts attention first and next;
- what feels resolved, specific, confusing, thin, repetitive, false, or
  unfinished;
- where the mobile and state adaptations strengthen or weaken the idea;
- what remains after a short delay.

Only then use the hypotheses below to name an observed cause. Do not show the
builder a motif catalog during initial direction work.

## Diagnose aggregates, not ingredients

A material finding needs an observed relationship, impact, and project
context. Useful aggregate causes include:

| Cause | Evidence in the render | Revision principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Irrelevance | Effects, labels, modules, media, or motion compete with the message or task and cannot explain their role. | Remove or reconnect the irrelevant relationship. |
| Repetition | Different content or routes inherit the same geometry, cadence, emphasis, interaction, or ending without a shared-system reason. | Rebuild the repeated organizing decision from each job. |
| Inconsistency | Type, media, spacing, control, or motion rules shift without meaning and make the work feel assembled. | Establish the local relationship that should stay coherent. |
| Falsity | Proof-shaped content, status, product UI, imagery, or claims imply facts or capability the project does not have. | Make it real, bound it as a concept, or remove the implication. |
| Incompleteness | Ordinary states, content pressure, responsive behavior, media, metadata, or interactions expose the first draft. | Finish the real path and its edge conditions. |
| Mismatch | A technically polished treatment belongs to another audience, era, genre, culture, or product relationship. | Re-derive from current project evidence. |
| Care residue | Defaults, placeholders, broken assets, console errors, accessibility failures, or unexplained dead controls remain. | Correct the implementation and rerun the affected evidence. |

A familiar card, gradient, serif, grotesk, mono label, centered hero, colored
word, count-up, asymmetry, stock photograph, glow, or component library is not
a finding by itself. An unusual choice is not proof of quality.

## Review common convergence hypotheses

These are non-exhaustive questions for a completed render. Preserve strong
counterexamples and record `not observed` when the candidate does not support
the hypothesis.

When a hypothesis needs frequency evidence rather than a category question,
consult the dated [observed default basins](quality/default-basins.md)
record: it names the exact coordinates where unguided model output has been
repeatedly seen to land (palettes, type postures, label furniture,
component finishes), so "was this derived or defaulted" can be asked
against specifics. It carries the same standing as every watch note here:
post-render evidence with counterexamples preserved, never a generation
constraint.

### Default substrate

Does the component library or starter theme remain recognizable because the
site kept its default token names, neutral scale, radius, border, icon, page
width, and component composition even though the subject calls for another
relationship? A genuine product or internal tool may intentionally inherit
that system. Re-theme or restructure only when the default substrate is the
observed mismatch.

### Swappable marketing formula

Does the same opening, feature grouping, proof sequence, conversion section,
and ending actually recur in owner-authorized unrelated output without a
project or system reason? Familiar sections can be correct when content
inventory and visitor questions produced them. The defect is observed
recurrence without evidence, not theoretical reuse by another industry or the
existence of a hero, cards, testimonials, FAQ, or CTA. Use [visual-grammar
variance](quality/visual-grammar-variance.md) for the comparison boundary.

### Decorative information costume

Do coordinates, serials, status dots, counters, diagrams, badges, or technical
labels look factual or operational without being real and relevant? They are
valid in technical, editorial, artistic, or operating contexts when their
meaning is truthful or clearly atmospheric. Revise false meaning, not the
visual category.

### Emphasis and label grammar

Does every headline isolate a word in the same color, face, italic, gradient,
or animation regardless of meaning? Does every section receive the same
eyebrow or index? A pre-heading line is not neutral scaffolding: ask what
visitor-useful fact it supplies that the heading does not. If it only announces
the subject, remove it rather than using a small label to make the section feel
designed. A treatment can be excellent when it has a real project role.
Repetition becomes a finding when it fragments reading, invents hierarchy, or
exposes a producer habit.

### Cardification and container grammar

Are unrelated ideas forced into identical rounded containers, icon rows,
stats, or bento tiles because the layout began from components instead of
content relationships? Cards remain appropriate for genuinely parallel,
selectable, movable, or comparable items. Change the information model, not
merely the border radius.

### Copy cadence

Do headings, fragments, list lengths, reassurance, punctuation, and CTA logic
repeat one rhetorical machine across sections or sites? Preserve deliberate
brand voice, taxonomy, safety repetition, and editorial rhythm. Do not replace
generic cadence with manufactured typos, random fragments, slang, or a new
formula.

### Media casting and generated artifacts

Does the imagery show only the statistical archetype of the subject, conflict
with the actual copy, or disagree as a set in light, lens, grade, era,
material, and representation? Inspect generated media for text, anatomy,
geometry, repetition, shadows, reflections, perspective, and cultural detail.
Preserve legitimate documentary variation. A photo-free or mixed-media
direction can pass when deliberate and convincing.

### Motion and interaction grammar

Does every element use the same reveal, spring, parallax, hover lift, or
cursor effect even when its job differs? Is motion delaying reading,
obscuring state, or substituting for composition? A repeated transition is
correct when it creates dependable system behavior. Revise motion that lacks
meaning, control, fallback, or project fit.

### Underdeveloped restraint or unsupported spectacle

Does the declared ambition fail because media, content, hierarchy, craft,
detail, or state depth is missing? Quiet work need not add spectacle; rich
work need not survive removal of its strongest medium. Judge the candidate
against its own stated target and owner feedback.

## Review typography without a font list

Inspect what painted, not only CSS declarations or family names. Compare the
complete rendered system:

- role assignment, scale, width, weight, case, punctuation, tracking, leading,
  wrapping, measure, and emphasis;
- language and script coverage, fallbacks, synthesis, loading, and failure;
- fit with audience, content, brand, era, density, and surrounding media;
- recurrence of the same role construction or emphasis grammar across
  unrelated projects.

Do not maintain a portable list of approved, overused, AI-associated, trendy,
or forbidden families. If dated research or an owner-authorized ledger notes
a family, use it only to ask whether habit displaced project fit. A common
family with a project-specific system can outperform a rare family chosen as
camouflage.

## Reopen convergence at its source

When a rendered project remains too close to an unrelated sibling, do not make
the fix an inverse prompt: no palette swap, cycling type choices, new shape, random
asymmetry, or added effect merely to prove difference. Reopen the earliest
shared decision that created the repeated encounter. That may be the visitor
occasion, opening model, dominant content unit, body progression, media role,
interaction purpose, or narrow-screen transformation.

Use [Project Contrast](quality/project-contrast.md) when the owner has asked
for cross-project distinction. It records a brief-native alternative before
the nearest sibling is consulted, separates justified shared foundations from
producer carryover, and binds wide and narrow evidence. The comparison produces
human review prompts, not a uniqueness score, authorship claim, or automatic
aesthetic pass.

## Separate builder residue from truthful implementation

Remove accidental public residue:

- default titles, descriptions, favicons, starter copy, placeholder routes,
  dead demo controls, sample tokens, unused dependencies, debug panels,
  prompt text, internal proof pages, and unowned analytics or network calls;
- false author, company, generator, product, security, performance, or
  capability metadata;
- broken source maps, missing assets, console errors, hydration failures, and
  exposed secrets.

Do not conceal truthful implementation merely to evade a detector. Normal
hashed filenames, framework directories, package metadata, accessible
semantics, source maps governed by the project's security policy, and honest
attribution are not visual slop. Preserve licenses and required provenance.

## Compare a batch of unrelated sites

For three or more independent sites, keep initial builds isolated from sibling
renders when practical. After each has an unprimed review:

1. freeze the exact source/build identities, matched wide/narrow captures, and
   authorized original evidence;
2. assign neutral specimen labels and withhold the label-to-site identity map
   through the first comparison observation. Neutral labels reduce identity
   priming; they are not pixel redaction. Only create a crop, redaction, or
   exclusion when it is authorized and needed for privacy, rights, or data
   minimization. Preserve the verified original within its authorized evidence
   boundary and record the transformation and resulting coverage loss;
3. compare opening geometry, route silhouettes, type roles, emphasis,
   containers, labels, icons, media relationships, motion, copy cadence,
   responsive transformation, and endings;
4. group repeated clusters and identify the closest sibling for each site;
5. ask whether brand, task, platform, genre, content, accessibility, or
   maintenance evidence explains the repetition;
6. revise only reproduced systemic causes, then rerender affected sites;
7. keep experiment history in a temporary batch record rather than polluting
   an accepted production ledger.

Do not claim that a neutral-label atlas conceals names, logos, copy, people,
URLs, media, or subject identity visible in its pixels. When a transformed copy
is necessary, compare only the relationships it still preserves and disclose
what can no longer be concluded.

Changed colors, fonts, copy, and photographs do not prove range when the
underlying encounter is unchanged. Conversely, justified shared foundations
do not make distinct sites the same.

## Maintain evidence without fossilizing taste

Research cards and dated watch notes must record source, sample, method,
retrieval date, confidence, limitations, counterexamples, review date, and the
exact hypothesis they support. Candidate or weak evidence stays a review
prompt and cannot create a pre-render ban or release gate.

Retire a watch item when current evidence no longer supports it. Preserve the
historical record for auditability, but do not keep teaching an expired trend
as timeless taste. Direct owner feedback about an exact render remains the
highest-value aesthetic evidence for that work and reopens the affected
decision without becoming a universal rule.
