# Design tuning

Use this when feedback asks for a directional change such as bolder, quieter,
clearer, warmer, more editorial, more playful, more cinematic, less generic,
or more polished. The words are evidence about the experienced result, not
commands for a predefined set of effects.

Read the [creative freedom boundary](../creative-freedom.md). Tuning changes
the decisions causing the observed result; it does not apply a Design DNA
house treatment.

## Bind the feedback

Before changing source, record:

- exact candidate/build, route, state, viewport, and region;
- the owner's or reviewer's words and decision authority;
- what they observed, expected, and rejected;
- accepted facts, tasks, assets, integrations, and visual qualities outside
  the affected scope;
- any missing condition that could change the diagnosis.

Direct accountable-owner feedback is first-party design evidence. A producer's
preferred interpretation does not outrank it. If the feedback refers to an
obsolete render, reproduce or bind the current condition before revising.

## Translate the request in context

Ask what the directional word means for this project and audience. “Bolder”
may concern confidence, scale, clarity, social energy, material presence,
contrast, wit, or another quality. “Quieter” may concern attention, tone,
reading, density, interaction, or something else. Do not silently map either
word to a font, palette, amount of whitespace, motion level, composition, or
component style.

Record the tuning contract:

| Feedback | Project-specific meaning and evidence | What should change observably | What must remain | Failure condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  | render, task, state, or audience response | accepted creative and hard constraints |  |

If the request is actually a rejection of the organizing direction, return to
[creative exploration](creative-exploration.md). A tuning pass cannot repair a
premise the owner no longer accepts.

## Choose the change from the candidate

Inspect the candidate's `creative_logic` and observable decision ledger. Find
the smallest coherent cause or group of causes whose revision can produce the
requested observation. The relevant decision may involve content,
composition, typography, media, color, material, ornament, interaction,
motion, density, convention, responsiveness, or another project-specific
concern.

The list is open. Do not require a particular medium, a single cause, or a
named tuning mode. Several coordinated decisions may need to change when they
form one system; one local decision may be enough when the issue is bounded.

Preserve aesthetic autonomy. Atmosphere, beauty, humor, ornament, restraint,
abundance, familiarity, and surprise can be legitimate goals. Revise them
because the rendered relationship fails the brief, not because they lack a
separate task or appear on a pattern list.

## Protect accepted work

Keep these stable unless the feedback or higher authority reopens them:

- approved facts, claims, privacy, rights, cultural, and operational
  boundaries;
- working tasks, integrations, routes, states, and recovery;
- accessibility and relevant responsive, input, language, preference, and
  failure behavior;
- verified brand and existing-system authority;
- accepted project-specific decisions outside the causal scope.

For an existing system, map the change to its real source. For a local defect,
do not regenerate the whole route and erase accepted work. For a reopened
direction, do not preserve a rejected system merely because it is already
implemented.

## Render and compare

Make the bounded coherent change, then compare the same relevant conditions.
Use real content and media dimensions when available. Record intentional drift
and inspect shared consumers when the cause is systemic.

Choose evidence that can answer the tuning contract. It may include desktop
and mobile renders, a reading specimen, a task state, a motion recording, a
content extreme, a localized view, or an unbriefed perception review. No fixed
viewport pair, effect-removal ritual, attention sequence, or recall test
applies to every pass.

When capability allows, record perception before revealing the rationale or
diagnostic vocabulary. Ask whether the requested change is visible, whether
the work still belongs to the project, what improved or regressed, and whether
the intended task or invitation remains intact.

For consequential motion, also verify controls, interruption, cancellation,
reduced behavior, unsupported runtime, and relevant input methods. For
typography, use actual copy, scripts, loading, fallback, and widths. These are
quality gates for the chosen medium, not instructions to choose it.

## Close or reopen

Close the tuning request when the exact rerender shows the intended change,
protected decisions remain intact, relevant regressions are cleared, and the
accountable reviewer accepts or dispositions the result at the assurance level
required.

Continue only when new evidence exposes another cause. Reopen the direction
when the owner rejects the organizing answer. Do not run an arbitrary number of
polish rounds, declare success from source changes alone, or replace the user's
feedback with a universal taste rule.
