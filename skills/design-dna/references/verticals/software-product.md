# Software product

Use this for SaaS, developer tools, AI products, platforms, APIs, and software marketing connected to an application.

## Route by real job

Marketing and comparison usually Persuade; documentation, changelogs, and specifications Read; signup, onboarding, configuration, daily work, billing, and administration Operate. Interactive demonstrations may Experience. Share product truth and identity while letting each route adopt the density and conventions its job needs.

Map the real workflow before designing feature sections:

- actor, object, input, action, result, and next step;
- roles, permissions, collaboration, ownership, and audit history;
- setup, integration, import, migration, and time-to-use;
- default, loading, empty, partial, stale, offline, error, success, and recovery;
- cancellation, export, deletion, support, and exit.

## Demonstrate the product truthfully

Use approved product captures, realistic labeled fixtures, or an explicitly identified prototype. Show a meaningful task and outcome rather than a collage of tiny dashboard panels.

- Keep screenshots current, legible, privacy-safe, and tied to the described capability.
- Distinguish available, beta, limited, planned, deprecated, and third-party behavior.
- Do not invent customers, quotes, usage, outcomes, uptime, integrations, certifications, or live activity.
- Avoid browser frames, terminal text, charts, and cursor animation that imply a working product when none exists.

Write from concrete user work: what the product changes, for whom, under what conditions, and with what limitation. Keep terminology stable between marketing, product, docs, and support. Replace generic transformation claims with demonstrable behavior and evidence.

## Support evaluation and procurement

Make applicable decision facts discoverable:

- pricing unit, billing period, included usage, overages, trial conditions, renewal, cancellation, and taxes;
- plan differences, eligibility, limits, migration, and support;
- integration direction, setup requirement, permission scope, data flow, and verified status;
- platform, browser, device, locale, export, retention, and portability constraints;
- security, privacy, accessibility, reliability, legal, and procurement contacts or documents that actually exist.

Treat `Contact sales`, waitlists, calculators, demos, and signup as real journeys with clear expectations, validation, privacy, success, failure, and handoff. Do not use a logo or badge as a substitute for the underlying approved evidence.

## Connect marketing and product

Carry naming, type, color roles, icon grammar, and component behavior across the boundary, then adapt density and emphasis to the task. Remove marketing theatrics from frequent workflows. Do not leave framework colors, fonts, radii, generic cards, empty states, or demo copy as the product's accidental identity.

Dark mode is contextual, not an automatic signal of technical credibility. Add it when environment, audience, task, or existing product support justifies the maintenance cost; test contrast, syntax, charts, focus, media, elevation, and system preference in both themes.

## Model agentic behavior

When software can generate, recommend, decide, or act on a user's behalf, expose the states and controls needed for its actual consequence: draft, awaiting input, awaiting approval, scheduled, running, paused, cancelled, partial, failed, completed, reverted, or superseded as applicable.

Clarify source material, permissions, action scope, cost or quota, freshness, uncertainty, human approval, external side effects, audit evidence, correction, cancellation, and recovery. Never imply monitoring, autonomy, certainty, or reversibility the system does not provide.

## Verify and escalate

Test the journey from claim to evidence to signup or contact, then through representative roles, data, states, responsive constraints, and failure recovery. Confirm that screenshots, docs, prices, limits, integration status, metadata, emails, and in-product language describe the same release.

Use qualified security, privacy, legal, accessibility, compliance, reliability, data, or AI-safety specialists when the product handles sensitive data, consequential automation, regulated decisions, enterprise attestations, or contractual claims. Do not infer readiness or certification from interface polish.
