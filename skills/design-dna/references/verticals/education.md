# Education and learning

Use this for schools, courses, learning platforms, training, assessment,
student information, credentials, and educational resources.

## Model the learning context

Identify:

- learner, educator, guardian, administrator, reviewer, and support roles;
- age, prior knowledge, language, accessibility, device, bandwidth, and
  environment;
- enrollment, prerequisites, schedule, attendance, progress, assessment,
  feedback, completion, and credential states;
- public, class, group, private, graded, draft, archived, and retained content;
- curriculum, instructor, accreditation, outcome, policy, and data owners.

Do not present sample enrollment, progress, grades, credentials, instructor
identity, outcomes, accreditation, or live classroom activity as real.

## Design for the learning goal

- Make objectives, prerequisites, sequence, workload, materials, and completion
  clear.
- Use examples, practice, feedback, retrieval, and reflection appropriate to
  the learning goal.
- Preserve learner position, attempts, drafts, notes, and recovery.
- Distinguish instruction, example, hint, answer, feedback, grade, and policy.
- Let users navigate and revisit without scroll traps or motion dependence.
- Provide transcripts, captions, descriptions, downloadable alternatives, and
  low-bandwidth paths where required.

Play, competition, ceremony, progression, narrative, spectacle, and
gamification are all available when the learning evidence and accountable
educator support them. Revise or remove a treatment when it distorts learning,
creates inappropriate pressure, excludes learners, or implies achievement the
system cannot support; do not reject it by ingredient alone.

## Handle assessment and feedback

State scoring, attempts, timing, accommodations, collaboration, use of tools,
submission, late policy, review, appeal, and release of results. Prevent
accidental submission and preserve work. Make automated or generated feedback
distinguishable from instructor judgment and expose correction or escalation.

Do not use interface polish to imply pedagogical validity, secure examination,
academic integrity, or recognized credential status.

## Protect learners

Minimize personal data and expose audience and sharing scope before posting.
For minors, guardianship, consent, communication, location, imagery,
moderation, and data handling require the organization's approved policy and
specialist review. Do not create covert monitoring, public ranking, behavioral
inference, or high-stakes automated decisions.

## Verify and escalate

Test new, returning, enrolled, waitlisted, incomplete, completed, failed,
expired, and withdrawn states; prerequisites; long content; save/resume;
timers; accommodations; feedback; attempt limits; offline/slow network;
keyboard, screen reader, zoom, captions, translated content, RTL, print, and
different roles.

Require educators, accessibility specialists, privacy and security experts,
legal counsel, safeguarding owners, assessment specialists, and accreditation
authorities for production claims and policy. Block release for incorrect
enrollment, grade, credential, learner-data, consent, or safety behavior.
