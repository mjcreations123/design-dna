# Editorial and publishing

Use this for magazines, news, journals, newsletters, blogs, reports, archives,
and other information products. Apply Read mode to comprehension and Persuade
or Operate only where subscription, membership, contribution, or management
becomes the primary job.

## Model the publication

Define:

- article, issue, series, topic, author, contributor, media, correction, and archive relationships;
- audience knowledge, reading length, entry source, and likely continuation;
- editorial status, owner, publication and update dates, version, and locale;
- citation, disclosure, sponsorship, moderation, and correction policies;
- free, registered, member, licensed, syndicated, or restricted access.

Let reporting form, evidence, media, audience, and reading task determine each
story's composition. Reuse a publication structure when those needs match; do
not impose one route silhouette on unlike material merely because it is easy
to template.

## Protect editorial trust

- Name authors, editors, photographers, illustrators, and sources only when approved.
- Separate reported fact, analysis, opinion, quotation, estimate, correction,
  advertisement, sponsored material, and generated or transformed media.
- Keep dates, timezones, update history, and material corrections visible.
- Preserve citations, captions, credits, transcript relationships, and source notes.
- Do not invent readership, awards, subscriber counts, quotations, or authority.
- Distinguish a machine-generated summary or translation when policy requires it.

## Build a reading and discovery system

Support useful headings, contents, deep links, search, topic and author paths,
related coverage, previous/next context, saved position, print, copy, and
citation according to the corpus. Avoid recommendation loops that obscure the
end of the piece or misrepresent relevance.

Design wide tables, figures, code, footnotes, galleries, audio, video, and live
updates as first-class content—not decorative interruptions.

## Handle access and participation

Explain registration, metering, membership, newsletter, comment, and
contribution consequences before collecting data. Preserve the reading context
through sign-in or payment. Make unsubscribe, preference, account, and support
paths discoverable. Do not disguise advertising, affiliate links, or
promotional capture as editorial navigation.

For comments or community contribution, apply the messaging and marketplace
trust boundaries appropriate to moderation, reporting, privacy, and deletion.

## Verify

Test direct entry, deep link, stale and corrected content, missing media,
extremely short and long articles, footnotes, citations, print, selection,
copy, search, keyboard, zoom, text spacing, screen reader, translated text,
RTL, paywall or sign-in interruption, subscription state, and offline or slow
loading where supported.

Block publication for material authorship, licensing, quotation, sponsorship,
correction, privacy, or access status that cannot be represented truthfully.
Use editorial, legal, accessibility, privacy, and subscription specialists for
their actual policies and regulated claims.
