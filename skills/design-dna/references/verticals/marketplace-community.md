# Marketplace and community

Use this for multi-sided listings, directories, exchanges, creator platforms,
member communities, reviews, reputation, and user-generated content.

## Model every side

Map buyer, seller, host, guest, creator, member, moderator, administrator,
support, and payment roles that actually exist. For each, identify:

- objects created, discovered, compared, saved, purchased, booked, or reported;
- identity, ownership, eligibility, permissions, and visibility;
- listing lifecycle and inventory or availability;
- transaction, fee, payout, cancellation, refund, and dispute boundaries;
- reputation, moderation, communication, and appeal;
- exit, data export, deletion, and retained obligations.

Do not optimize the discovery grid while leaving listing creation, failure,
support, or the other side of the exchange undefined.

## Preserve listing and reputation truth

- Separate seller claims, platform verification, sponsored placement, reviews,
  and editorial recommendations.
- Explain price, fees, taxes, availability, condition, delivery, location,
  cancellation, and eligibility before commitment.
- Identify review author, transaction relationship, date, edits, incentives,
  aggregation, and moderation where material.
- Do not invent verified badges, popularity, demand, ratings, live viewers,
  scarcity, safety, or guarantee.
- Make ranking, promotion, and recommendation labels understandable where
  product and policy require them.

## Support trust and recourse

Design real report, block, mute, dispute, refund, appeal, and support paths.
State evidence requirements, timing, scope, privacy, and expected next step
without promising an outcome. Preserve records needed for recovery while
minimizing exposure of private information.

Moderation status should distinguish received, under review, actioned, closed,
appealed, and unavailable only when operations support those states.

## Handle participation safely

Provide clear content rules, audience and visibility controls, draft and
preview, upload progress, validation, attribution, edit and deletion behavior,
and consequences before publishing. Make public, members-only, group,
unlisted, and private scope difficult to confuse.

For direct messaging and notifications, apply the messaging lifecycle. For
payment and recurring plans, apply ecommerce and subscription boundaries.

## Verify and escalate

Test both sides with new, established, restricted, suspended, and deleted
accounts; empty and dense results; unavailable listings; stale inventory;
payment and payout failure; duplicate submission; cancellation; dispute;
reporting; blocking; moderation; permission changes; private content; keyboard,
touch, zoom, screen reader, localization, and slow networks.

Require trust-and-safety, legal, privacy, security, payments, tax, identity,
fraud, and operations specialists for production policies and claims. Block
release when apparently active transactions, verification, moderation, or
guarantees are only visual simulations.
