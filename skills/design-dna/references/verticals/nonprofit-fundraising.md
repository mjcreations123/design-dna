# Nonprofit and fundraising

Use this for charities, foundations, advocacy, mutual aid, cultural
institutions, campaigns, grants, membership, volunteering, and donations.

## Ground the mission

Collect approved:

- organization identity, jurisdiction, status, mission, programs, and audience;
- current need, geography, eligibility, partners, and operational limits;
- impact measures with method, period, denominator, and owner;
- use of funds, restrictions, fees, matching terms, and tax treatment;
- consented stories, imagery, credits, privacy boundaries, and safeguarding
  rules;
- ways to donate, volunteer, attend, advocate, learn, or request support.

Do not invent urgency, beneficiaries, impact, tax deductibility, matching
deadlines, partner logos, financial ratios, or registration status.

## Represent people with dignity

Use consented, context-rich material. Avoid dehumanizing scarcity, savior
narratives, staged distress, visual stereotypes, and imagery whose emotional
force outruns its truth. Protect names, locations, health, immigration,
financial, and minors-related information. Offer anonymization only under the
organization's real safeguarding policy.

## Make support choices clear

- Distinguish one-time, recurring, designated, unrestricted, tribute, ticket,
  membership, pledge, and in-kind support.
- State amount, frequency, currency, fees, allocation, cancellation, receipt,
  tax, privacy, and communication consequences before submission.
- Explain preset amounts only when their impact labels are sourced.
- Keep optional tip and marketing consent separate from the donation.
- Provide equitable non-digital or assistance paths when the organization
  supports them.

Apply the subscription lifecycle to recurring giving and the ecommerce
lifecycle to merchandise or ticket sales.

## Show impact honestly

Connect claims to programs, dates, populations, methods, and limitations.
Separate target, estimate, output, outcome, and attributed story. Make reports,
financials, governance, and contact paths discoverable when available. Do not
replace substantive accountability with proof-shaped decoration or implied
authority, regardless of its visual form.

## Verify and escalate

Test one-time and recurring paths, custom and extreme amounts, fees, currency,
tribute details, matching eligibility, restricted-fund selection, payment
failure, duplicate prevention, cancellation, receipt, privacy preferences,
screen reader, keyboard, zoom, mobile, localization, and third-party handoff.

Require nonprofit, fundraising, safeguarding, legal, tax, privacy, security,
payments, accessibility, and communications specialists for production rules.
Block release for unsupported impact, status, tax, matching, allocation, or
apparently live donation claims.
