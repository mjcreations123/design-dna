# Local business and hospitality

Use this for coffee shops, restaurants, bars, bakeries, salons, studios, clinics, trades, venues, and other place- or service-based businesses.

## Serve the real visit or service journey

Prioritize what a customer needs to decide and act:

- what is offered and for whom;
- location, service area, access, and transport;
- current hours and holiday exceptions;
- price or qualification;
- menu, services, availability, or booking;
- dietary, allergen, accessibility, age, or preparation information;
- contact and verified social channels;
- cancellation, delivery, pickup, refund, or other relevant policy.

Route by task: the home page usually Persuades, menu or service detail Reads, galleries may Experience, and ordering or booking Operates.

## Gather specific material

Ask for the highest-leverage missing inputs, such as:

- current logo, signage, interior, packaging, uniforms, menus, and photography;
- neighborhood, building, landscape, clientele, and service rhythm;
- signature products or techniques;
- voice and approved claims;
- real constraints that distinguish the visit.

If these are unavailable, create a clearly labeled, reversible concept. Do not invent a founder story, address, hours, menu, sourcing practice, accessibility feature, event, review, rating, or amenity.

## Direct without category costume

A coffee shop is not automatically sepia, chalkboard, kraft paper, script type, coffee beans, faux-European, industrial, or mid-century. Nor is it automatically cream, display serif, sage green, oversized editorial photography, and pill buttons.

Build from the actual place and offer. A current result may be quiet, colorful, utilitarian, editorial, playful, or experimental. Vintage or heritage cues are valid when real evidence supports them.

## Handle menus and service data

- Use structured, owner-editable data when possible.
- Keep names, descriptions, prices, variants, availability, and dietary/allergen information distinct.
- Do not communicate dietary or allergen meaning by color or icon alone.
- Show when information was last confirmed.
- Provide a printable or low-bandwidth form when useful.
- Make temporary unavailability and seasonal change easy to update.

## Implement integrations honestly

Do not activate maps, reservations, ordering, payments, email capture, analytics, pixels, or cookie-setting embeds without approval and real configuration. When a third party owns the transaction, explain the handoff and preserve a safe return path.

## Prove the actual journey

Select only the branches the business really supports:

- **Visit, menu, or retail:** test finding current hours, address, access,
  availability, price, and the visit, pickup, or order handoff; verify every
  depicted product, place, map pin, dietary statement, and holiday exception.
- **Appointment:** test eligibility, service choice, duration or price
  uncertainty, preparation, availability, confirmation, rescheduling,
  cancellation, reminders, privacy, and a safe return from any third party.
- **Quote or service area:** test address or area qualification, scope
  questions, evidence upload only when authorized, estimate boundaries,
  response expectations, escalation, and what happens when the job is outside
  scope. Do not imply a price, arrival time, or staffed area that is unverified.
- **Urgent or emergency service:** distinguish genuine staffed availability
  from ordinary lead capture; make safety, hours, response limits, location,
  and alternative emergency instructions explicit and owner-approved.
- **Clinic or regulated service:** verify practitioner identity, credentials,
  jurisdiction, scope, risk and emergency boundaries, privacy behavior,
  accessibility, and required specialist legal or clinical review.
- **Event or venue:** test date and time, capacity, admission, age or access
  limits, transit, accessibility, weather, schedule changes, cancellation, and
  what the ticket or inquiry action actually does.

Across the selected journey, review mobile reachability, glare, slow networks,
one-handed use, zoom, long content, stale information, closed/unavailable
states, and recovery. Ensure the design does not imply an earlier decade unless
that time register is deliberate and documented.

## Release blockers

Block public release for unconfirmed contact details, prices, hours, policies, legal/food safety claims, unlicensed assets, fabricated proof, or an apparently live transaction that is only a mockup.
