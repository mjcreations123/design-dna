# Observed default basins

Use this only after a candidate is rendered and has received an unprimed
observation, as a companion to the
[post-render convergence review](../convergence-watch.md). It is a dated
evidence record of where unguided model output has been repeatedly observed
to land: the attractor basins of the statistical prior. Its one job is to
answer a review question the convergence review raises but cannot settle
alone: **was this decision derived from the project, or did the producer's
prior make it?**

This file is not a ban list, a style guide, or a generation-time constraint.
A candidate that lands in a basin is not failed for the coordinates; it is
asked for its derivation. When project evidence, brand direction, or an
owner decision independently supports the same choice, record the
counterexample and pass it. When no derivation exists beyond "this is what
came out," the basin is evidence that the prior chose, and the decision is
re-derived from the project. Never consult this file while generating; a
basin list read before the candidate exists becomes an inverse prompt, and
the complement of a known basin is as guessable as the basin.

Compiled 2026-08-22 from four source classes: production-round harvests
published by prompt-engineering practitioners (tasteskill, retrieved
2026-08-22), interface-guideline corpora (Vercel Web Interface Guidelines,
retrieved 2026-08-22), a public corpus of reverse-engineered brand token
records (VoltAgent awesome-design-md, 74 files, retrieved 2026-08-22), and
this studio's own dated owner-rejection record. Confidence varies per entry
and is stated. Review by 2027-02: model priors move with training data, and
an expired basin taught as timeless taste is itself a producer fingerprint.

## How to use a basin entry

For each basin the rendered candidate appears to occupy:

1. name the observed coordinates in the render, exactly;
2. ask what project evidence chose them: brief, brand asset, audience,
   content, platform, or recorded owner decision;
3. record `derived` with the evidence, or `defaulted` with no evidence;
4. for `defaulted`, reopen the decision from the project. Do not swap to the
   basin's opposite; the opposite of a known default is the second-most
   predictable output.

A single basin hit is weak evidence. Several simultaneous hits with no
derivation is the strong signal, because independent project-derived
decisions rarely co-locate with the prior across palette, type, labels,
and components at once.

## Palette basins

**The generator hues.** High confidence; corroborated across scanner
corpora, practitioner harvests, and this studio's own review history.
Unguided output gravitates to an indigo-violet band as accent, gradient, or
glow: observed centers include #6366F1, #4F46E5, #8B5CF6, #7C3AED, #A855F7,
#9333EA, #3B82F6, #2563EB on slate grounds near #1E293B, #0F172A, #020617.
These are default utility-framework primaries; their recurrence reflects
training data, not a judgment about the hues. A brand that genuinely owns
violet keeps it, with the derivation recorded.

**The premium-consumer basin.** High confidence; harvested from repeated
production rounds by an independent practitioner and consistent with this
studio's own rejected work. When asked for "premium," "artisan," or
"quiet luxury," unguided output repeatedly lands on warm paper grounds
(observed centers #F5F1EA, #F7F5F1, #FBF8F1, #EFEAE0, #ECE6DB, #E8DFCB),
brass, clay, oxblood, or ochre accents (observed centers #B08947, #B6553A,
#9A2436, #9C6E2A, #BC7C3A), and espresso ink (#1A1714 family). Each value
is a defensible choice; the basin is the *combination arriving unprompted*
for every premium brief, which makes unrelated clients' sites converge.
The studio's own dated gold finding is the same mechanism: the tell was
never the hue, it was the default doing every job.

**Related mechanism, lower specificity:** a single desaturated accent on a
zinc/slate neutral ground is the most common corrective basin, produced by
producers steering away from the generator hues. Escaping one basin into
the adjacent one is not derivation.

## Type-posture basins

**The display-serif attractor.** High confidence for the two named centers.
When a brief says editorial, premium, or warm, unguided output reaches for
a small set of display serifs far more often than human portfolios do;
practitioner production rounds repeatedly name **Fraunces** and
**Instrument Serif** as the two most frequent, with Playfair Display the
older center of the same basin. None of these faces is a finding; each has
shipped excellent human work. The review question is whether the face was
compared against alternatives with project reasons (the typography
protocol's selection evidence) or arrived because the prior associates
"tasteful serif" with these files. This studio has observed the pull
directly: a producer reached for Instrument Serif unprompted in a timed
build the same month this record was compiled.

**Compression posture.** Medium confidence. Oversized lowercase display at
tight negative tracking above a small weak subline, and the giant-heading /
tiny-caption pairing, recur across unguided output regardless of subject.
Judge scale relationships against the content's actual hierarchy per the
typography reference; the basin is the posture arriving before the content
was weighed.

## Label and micro-text basins

High confidence; the richest harvest in the production-round corpus, and
the class this studio's owner record already closes several members of.
Recurring micro-text furniture observed in unguided output:

- section-number eyebrows (`00 / INDEX`, `001 · CAPABILITIES`) and
  tile pagination (`01 / 4`) on non-sequential content;
- version and build labels in marketing heroes (`V0.6`, `BETA`,
  `INVITE-ONLY PREVIEW`) and version footers (`v0.6.2-rc.1`,
  `last sync 4s ago`) on pages with no versioned product;
- pulsing or colored status dots and live/connected badges on surfaces with
  no operational state to report;
- locale and weather strips (`LIS 14:23 · 18°C`) on briefs with no
  geographic task;
- poetic craftsman labels (`Field notes`, `On our desks`, `Loose plates`)
  standing in for real category names;
- photo-credit-shaped decoration (`Frame XII · 35mm`) crediting nothing;
- hero-bottom word strips (`BRAND. MOTION. SPATIAL.`) and labeled scroll
  cues; middle-dot chains as universal separators;
- decorative code, coordinates, serials, and crosshair or hairline-grid
  ornament implying instrumentation that does not exist.

Most of these are already covered as truth or parseability failures when
the implied fact is false: the [parseable text](parseable-text.md) gate and
the assurance boundaries govern that. This record adds the frequency
evidence: their *co-occurrence* is a strong prior signature even when each
instance could be defended alone. Genuine nomenclature, real operational
status on an operator surface, and true editorial metadata all remain open,
per the convergence review's decorative-information hypothesis.

## Component and surface basins

Medium-to-high confidence, multiple corroborating corpora:

- the fully rounded capsule action with a trailing arrow glyph as the
  default call to action (this studio's owner record closes it for studio
  work; the basin evidence is that it recurs industry-wide in unguided
  output);
- identical rounded containers for unrelated content, containers nested in
  containers, and the equal-thirds icon-heading-blurb benefit row;
- layered soft shadows, semi-transparent hairline borders, and
  background-hue-tinted depth applied as a universal finish regardless of
  the direction's material logic (a published guideline corpus recommends
  exactly this finish as a default, which is how a recommendation becomes
  a basin);
- glassmorphic blur panels and gradient-filled display text as unprompted
  premium signifiers;
- the left-text right-image split repeated as every section's geometry, and
  grids padded with empty decorative cells to complete a shape the content
  does not fill;
- div-built imitation product interfaces standing in for real screenshots,
  the single most-cited implementation tell in the harvested corpus (also a
  falsity finding under the assurance boundaries when it implies a product
  state that does not exist).

Each ingredient remains neutral alone. A familiar card, gradient, capsule,
or split is not a finding by itself; the basin evidence bears only on
derivation.

## Copy and content basins

High confidence; consistent across independent harvests and this studio's
history. Recurring unguided-copy signatures: the elevate/seamless/unleash/
next-gen/transformative vocabulary band; invented round-number proof
(`99.99%`, `10,000+ customers`) and its inverse tell, fake-organic
precision (`47.2%`) manufactured to look measured; placeholder-brand names
(Acme, Nexus, NovaCore) and placeholder people; mock-humble and
performative-craftsman sentences; cute wordplay that does not parse on a
second read. Fabricated proof is already an assurance-boundary violation
regardless of frequency; this record adds that the *voice pattern* recurs
even when the facts are true, which is a specificity finding. Route copy
findings through the [parseable text](parseable-text.md) gate and the
microcopy self-audit rather than a phrase blacklist.

## Brand-record ground truth

A public corpus of reverse-engineered brand token records
(`VoltAgent/awesome-design-md`, 74 files, MIT, retrieved 2026-08-22)
machine-encodes the exact palettes, type scales, radii, and component
grammars of the brands unguided output most often converges toward: Linear,
Vercel, Stripe, Apple, Notion, and the dark-ground huge-type mono-eyebrow
developer-site face among them. Its intended use, copying a famous brand's
language into a new project, is the opposite of this skill's method and is
not adopted. Its evidentiary use is legitimate: when a rendered candidate
feels like a specific brand's clone, fetch that brand's record
(`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/main/design-md/<slug>/DESIGN.md`)
and compare tokens and moves, turning "this feels like Linear" into named,
checkable coordinates. Fetch on demand; do not vendor brand aesthetics into
the skill or a project.

## Maintain this record

Each entry above states its hypothesis, source class, retrieval date, and
confidence, per the [evidence policy](../evidence.md). Add a basin only
with corroborated observation across independent sources or repeated dated
studio evidence; retire an entry when current output no longer lands there,
preserving the historical note. Owner rejections stay in the owner's own
standards record with their dates; this file may cite that record as
corroboration but never absorbs its authority. No entry here creates a
pre-render ban, a release gate, or a score.
