# Observed convergence prompts

Use this only after a candidate has been rendered and received an unprimed
observation, as a companion to the
[post-render convergence review](../convergence-watch.md). It supplies
cause-oriented prompts for a reviewer who suspects that several unrelated
decisions arrived from a familiar production prior rather than this project.

This is not a ban list, a style guide, an AI detector, or a prevalence claim.
No color, typeface, shape, component, sentence form, layout, or effect is a
finding by category. Never consult this file while generating: reading an
anti-default vocabulary before a direction exists merely turns its complement
into another predictable recipe.

Compiled 2026-08-23 from a bounded review of source methods and project
experience. The package's maintainer evidence records preserve the pinned
sources, revision dates, counterexamples, and limits. Those sources demonstrate
that tools and guidance can encourage reusable defaults; they do not establish
how often any ingredient occurs across all generated websites. Review this
record by 2027-02-23 because production priors and interface conventions move.

## Use the prompt, not its opposite

For a suspected convergence cluster:

1. name the exact rendered decisions without guessing who or what made them;
2. identify the brief, brand, audience, content, platform, owner, or measured
   behavior that selected each decision;
3. record `derived` with that evidence, `defaulted` when no project cause can
   be found, or `uncertain` when the review cannot decide;
4. preserve a project-serving choice as a counterexample even when it is
   familiar;
5. reopen a defaulted decision from the content and encounter, not by swapping
   it for the fashionable opposite.

A single familiar choice is weak evidence. A cluster across unrelated layers
can justify investigating the common cause, but it still does not prove
authorship, low quality, or a required replacement.

## Public-text furniture

Inspect small visible strings that appear to make a composition feel authored:
section numbers, eyebrows, version labels, status dots, place/time strips,
credits, coordinates, serials, scroll cues, or instrument-like annotations.
The question is whether each string conveys an independent visitor-useful fact,
state, source, sequence, or accepted editorial convention. If it only restates
the adjacent heading, narrates production, or implies a system that does not
exist, remove or translate it through the [parseable-text](parseable-text.md)
and [public-copy](../craft/public-copy.md) boundaries. Genuine metadata and
operational status remain valid.

## Repeated section grammar

Compare section jobs before comparing their furniture. Suspect defaulting when
unrelated sections or routes repeatedly receive the same container, split,
icon-heading-blurb unit, heading cadence, action placement, or narrow stacking
response even though their content relationships differ. Reuse is valid when a
real component system, task continuity, or maintenance contract explains it.
The repair is to restore each section's content operation while preserving
justified shared foundations.

## Prestige without a project cause

Inspect any cluster used mainly to signal premium, technical, editorial,
friendly, futuristic, or handcrafted character. The cluster may involve
palette, type posture, depth, blur, grain, motion, cropping, density, or
ornament. Every ingredient remains open. Ask what project evidence chose the
combination and whether it survives real content, states, and responsive
conditions. Do not replace a familiar prestige code with a mandated minimalist
or maximalist code.

## Implied systems and proof

Treat imitation dashboards, fabricated metrics, decorative controls, fake
activity, invented testimonials, and product-shaped diagrams as truth and
behavior risks before treating them as visual tells. A real product screenshot,
honest local fixture, clearly illustrative model, or documentary proof can use
familiar interface language. A decorative simulation must not imply data,
operation, affiliation, or availability that the project cannot support.

## Portable copy

Inspect slogans, reassurance, benefit abstractions, polished contrast formulas,
vague attribution, synonym cycling, repeated triads, and generic uplift only
after factual copy exists. The useful test is portability: could the line move
unchanged to an unrelated organization without losing meaning? If so, replace
it with a sourced fact, mechanism, consequence, scene, voice-bearing detail, or
real next action when one exists. Keep intentional rhythm and punctuation; do
not create a new anti-slop dialect. Use [public copy and voice](../craft/public-copy.md).

## Close a finding

Record:

- the render, route, state, and viewport observed;
- the cluster rather than one isolated ingredient;
- the project derivation or its absence;
- counterevidence and accepted conventions;
- the earliest content, encounter, or system decision reopened;
- the rerendered result and whether the same cause remains.

This record may prompt a contextual revision. It cannot issue a score, declare
originality, infer AI use, or turn a dated observation into a permanent taste
law.
