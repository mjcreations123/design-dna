# Project Contrast

Use Project Contrast when an accountable owner says an unrelated public build
must differ from recent authorized work, identifies a repeated answer that
needs correction, or authorizes a comparison after concrete recurrence
evidence. A Showcase, portfolio sample, demo, pitch, or evaluation can prompt
an authorized comparison decision; none activates this capability by itself.
Project Contrast introduces a small amount of productive friction before code
turns one plausible answer into another polished version of the same site.

It is not a style roulette, a font or palette rotation, an authorship detector,
an aesthetic score, or a promise that every viewer will agree a result is
beautiful. It requires different **derivation**, not arbitrary decoration.

## When to activate

Activate this capability for a new unrelated public site when the accountable
owner does one of the following:

- says recent projects feel alike, asks for a distinctly different answer, or
  activates an owner-scoped recurrence requirement;
- directs a response to a prior result rejected for reusing structure under new
  words, imagery, or surface styling; or
- authorizes comparison after a documented evaluation identifies an unexplained
  repeated encounter across otherwise unrelated outputs.

A Showcase, portfolio sample, pitch, client-facing demo, or evaluation that
may be judged beside recent studio work can prompt the question of whether an
authorized comparison is material. It does not activate Project Contrast by
itself. If no accountable owner selects that comparison, continue with the
capability otherwise required by the brief and record the comparison boundary
only when it affects a later review.

Do not activate it to force novelty inside an approved brand system, a
maintenance task, a bounded component repair, or a product family whose
repetition has a real task, identity, platform, or maintenance basis. Record
that limitation instead of pretending the work is unrelated.

When the owner expressly escalates this concern with
`owner-recurrence-requirement`, pair Project Contrast with
[Direction Challenge](direction-challenge.md). Project Contrast still records
the authorized lineage and collision test; Direction Challenge independently
proves that the current brief produced three incompatible roots and two
cross-root rendered slices before broad implementation. That stronger workflow
belongs only to the declared escalation. Project Contrast by itself continues
to use a proportional counter-answer rather than a fixed concept count.

## Research basis and limits

This is a process safeguard, not a theory that can identify an AI-made site.
Studies in adjacent generative ideation tasks have found a useful tension:
assistance may improve an individual's local result while making a collection
of results more alike, and high-fidelity generated examples can encourage
fixation. See [Doshi and Hauser's 2024 study](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290),
[Anderson, Shah, and Kreminski's group-level analysis](https://mkremins.github.io/publications/Homogenization_CHI2024.pdf),
and [Wadinambiarachchi et al.'s 2024 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11164).
Those studies do not prove that any particular website is homogeneous or ugly.
They support a modest intervention: challenge the first plausible answer,
keep project evidence visible, and inspect a rendered result before it becomes
a reusable house grammar.

[Design Heuristics research](https://dl.designresearchsociety.org/drs-conference-papers/drs2018/researchpapers/200/)
supports materially different prompts as a way to generate less-similar
concepts. This skill therefore asks for a counter-answer at the level of the
public encounter, not arbitrary changes to colors, typefaces, shapes, or
effects.

## What stays shared

Project Contrast separates reliable foundations from the project-owned public
encounter. Semantic navigation, keyboard behavior, access, privacy, truth,
error handling, analytics boundaries, platform primitives, established brand
rules, and legal or operating requirements may remain shared. Reuse is not a
failure when it is explained.

Do not let a shared foundation expand silently into a shared page recipe. The
opening, dominant content operation, body progression, media relationship,
type behavior, task interaction, mobile transformation, and ending remain
available to the current project. They may repeat only when the record states
why that relationship is correct here.

When the owner names repeated hero, CTA, card, panel, background, underline,
font-pairing, or route-shell behavior, use [visual-grammar
variance](visual-grammar-variance.md) to inspect the rendered relationships.
The comparison is against actual owner-authorized output, not an imagined test
of whether another industry could occupy the layout. A design can be
transferable and still be project-derived; it fails contrast when the same
producer grammar actually recurs without cause.

## Derive before comparing

Start from the current project without viewing prior site output. Record only
the evidence that can change the public encounter:

- visitor occasion, primary job, and the decision, understanding, or response
  the surface should support;
- real subject material, operating reality, tension, place, object, ritual,
  process, data, language, or task that can carry form;
- content operation: for example, orient, choose, compare, read, plan,
  inspect, transact, participate, or another project-specific operation;
- relevant facts, rights, accessibility, system, brand, delivery, and
  maintenance constraints;
- the proposed organizing answer in ordinary project language.

Do not begin with a style name, component inventory, trend list, or the
previous client's screenshots. A brief can honestly support a quiet, dense,
conventional, theatrical, typographic, photographic, task-led, spatial, or
otherwise singular answer.

## Use the record lifecycle

`project-contrast.json` begins as an explicit `draft`: project-derived fields
are `null` or empty rather than plausible English placeholders. A draft is a
truthful planning artifact, not a clean Project Contrast pass.

Move the record forward only as its evidence exists:

- `draft` — no project-specific direction has been claimed yet;
- `direction-ready` — the visitor occasion, source-to-encounter chain,
  selected direction, counter-answer, signature basis, and three review prompts
  are written in project language;
- `proof-ready` — an exact candidate build, project-relevant render review,
  and wide/narrow captures are bound; and
- `reviewed` — the unprimed and applicable paired review, counterfactual, and
  disposition are recorded. A completed unprimed review names its relationship,
  neutral candidate-capture-only exposure, first observation, and exact bound
  capture IDs; a self-review may be retained but does not satisfy this
  independent-review stage. A completed paired review names its candidate and
  authorized comparator IDs after the unprimed review was frozen, exposes the
  complete declared wide/narrow capture set, and records a structured
  `not-interchangeable`, `still-too-close`, or `inconclusive` result. A
  `still-too-close` result reopens work; it cannot be hidden behind an accepted
  disposition. Any completed owner review is recorded separately and bound to
  the exact candidate build and reviewed capture
  IDs.

The initializer may create a draft without pretending it is complete. Its
`--check-state` result visibly warns on any Project Contrast draft and fails
when an explicit `owner-recurrence-requirement` remains draft. The full audit
and readiness checks still validate all claims; lifecycle status never
certifies visual quality, originality, or human authorship.

`--migrate` converts only the known older packaged placeholder record into an
explicit lifecycle draft and keeps a hash-bound migration entry. It deliberately
does not guess how to reinterpret a partially completed legacy record; update
that record with the accountable project owner instead.

## Record a project-derived signature

Before implementation spreads a plausible answer across a site, record the
qualitative axes that materially shape this project. Give each selected axis a
project-defined identifier and group it as either `encounter` or
`surface-language`; the identifier is not chosen from a global catalog. The
record is not a checklist that every site must fill or vary. An axis can refer
to the current project's opening, information operation, spatial model,
density, type behavior, media relationship, interaction purpose, mobile
transformation, or another material decision.

An axis may be `not-applicable`, intentionally quiet, or shared with an
approved system. A change in one surface axis does not establish a new public
encounter. The record asks what those decisions cause a visitor to do,
understand, or feel in this project, then lets a reviewer inspect that claim in
the rendered work.

When `owner-recurrence-requirement` is active, select a nonempty material set
from both project-defined groups:

- at least one **encounter** axis; and
- at least one **surface-language** axis.

This does not dictate a font, palette, geometry, component, or effect. It
prevents a contrast record from treating one cosmetic observation as evidence
that the entire encounter has changed.

## Separate public foundations from public shells

Name a shared boundary accurately before calling it reusable. The contract
offers three qualitative classifications:

- `technical-foundation` for semantic landmarks, keyboard behavior, privacy
  mechanics, operating primitives, or other non-public implementation
  foundations. It must not contain an observation of visible public chrome or
  rendered public-shell evidence.
- `approved-public-system` for visible navigation, identity, or operating
  chrome that the accountable owner has already approved as a system. Bind a
  complete candidate wide/narrow pair, a hash-bound owner approval artifact,
  and a deliberately narrow approval scope. That scope approves only the
  named visible system boundary; it does not silently pre-approve the current
  page opening, body, media role, action path, or campaign language.
- `candidate-public-shell` for visible chrome that may itself be recurring or
  project-specific. Bind candidate and authorized comparator evidence, then
  review it separately rather than burying it inside a technical label. Its
  collision claim needs an observed or revised `different` or
  `shared-with-reason` relationship with bound candidate and comparator IDs.
  `not-comparable` is an honest limitation, not a cleared collision gate, and
  prose alone is not a public-shell evidence binding.

This is not a demand that navigation or identity must change. It prevents an
otherwise identical public frame from silently carrying a repeated campaign
recipe into unrelated work.

## Bind surface grammar to rendered evidence

When an owner recurrence requirement is active, identify the selected surface
axes in project language and record observations that a reviewer can actually
inspect. An observation has a project-defined label, the selected axis or axes
it concerns, candidate and comparator observations, a project cause, source,
status, and references to hash-bound candidate captures and authorized
comparator evidence.

Do not turn this into a fixed list of ingredients. A record may concern a
reading behavior, photographic distance, material treatment, contrast field,
or another project-specific public signal. It does not require a particular
font, palette, edge treatment, geometry, texture, animation, or quantity of
variation. A bright hard-edged public register can be accepted when it follows
the source and is evidenced; a quiet surface can be accepted for the same
reason.

For selected surface axes, a readiness claim needs an actual `observed`,
`revised`, or reviewed-accepted comparison with bound evidence and an
authorized comparator relationship: `different` or `shared-with-reason`.
`not-comparable` is valid candidate-only documentation, but cannot count as
selected-axis comparison evidence for an owner recurrence requirement. If an
axis is intentionally shared, use `shared-with-reason` and bind the source and
evidence instead of writing an uncheckable exception.

An accepted `different` claim at the encounter, opening, body, content-unit,
or mobile-encounter level is stronger than a reviewer sentence. It must bind a
structured candidate-capture set and an authorized comparator artifact. The
candidate captures resolve through the schema-3 report rather than through
standalone image bytes; a mobile-encounter claim must include a bound narrow
capture. This identifies what a reviewer inspected without prescribing the
font, color, geometry, or motion that made the project appropriate.

For an owner-recurrence requirement, the accepted structural claim must bind
the verified comparator marked `closest-sibling`. A different authorized
comparator can add diagnostic context, but cannot substitute for the nearest
prior answer whose recurrence is at issue. Selected surface observations and a
candidate public-shell collision likewise count only when they bind that
closest sibling.

For a `different` relationship, an image comparator cannot reuse the exact
bytes of any verified candidate capture. The auditor rejects that literal
pixel reuse; it does not attempt to score near-similarity or aesthetic
originality. An image comparator also names its own hash-bound source build,
route, state, browser viewport, and full-page image extent. A tiny image,
thumbnail, or crop cannot pose as whole-route public evidence. This metadata
does not claim that the audit can independently reconstruct an external
browser session; it makes the retained comparison boundary inspectable.

A structural abstract remains an explicitly limited, privacy-proportionate
record for structural comparison. It can support a comparison of encounter,
opening, body, content unit, or mobile operation, but cannot support a
`different` claim about visible public-shell or surface-language grammar. Use
actual, bounded image evidence for those visible public relationships.

## Challenge the first answer

Name the likely first answer before it disappears into implementation. Create
one **materially different counter-answer** from the same approved brief and
source material when recurrence risk or direction uncertainty is high. It can
be a content model, annotated wireframe, proof fragment, interaction prototype,
reference decomposition, or representative route. Its form and fidelity follow
the decision; no fixed candidate count applies.

The counter-answer must alter the encounter or the operation that structures
the body. Examples of consequential differences include an entry through a
choice rather than a statement, an artifact rather than a promotional surface,
a reading sequence rather than a parallel feature grouping, a plan or map
rather than a long scroll, or a task state rather than an editorial opening.
These are examples, not mandatory archetypes.

A different family, accent, image crop, shape, effect, or motion treatment
inside the same opening, section sequence, content grouping, action logic, and
ending is normally a reskin. It may be an excellent local alternative, but it
does not settle a recurrence concern by itself.

For an active owner-recurrence requirement, `not-needed` cannot stand in for
the counter-answer, and a verbatim copy of the selected organizing answer is
not a counter-answer. Record the concrete encounter, operation, or body model
that changed. This is a narrow anti-fixation evidence rule, not a prescribed
number of concepts or a visual-style requirement.

Select for the project, not for strangeness. Record why the selected answer
fits the visitor, material, task, voice, accessibility, production reality,
and owner preference better than the counter-answer. Keep a reversible
checkpoint until the riskiest representative slice has been rendered.

Before proof, write three concise, project-specific review prompts in the record:

- one encounter-collision prompt that asks whether the opening, operation,
  progression, or task path remains too close after subject material is
  mentally removed; and
- one public-shell-collision prompt that asks whether persistent visible chrome
  has become an unexplained shared campaign frame; and
- one surface-grammar prompt that asks whether rendered type, media, material,
  or another project-defined signal supports this project rather than merely
  decorating the same structure.

Prompts direct human inspection. They are not bans, do not prescribe a
difference in every ingredient, and cannot be closed by rotating a font,
palette, shape, image crop, or effect on otherwise unchanged structure.

## Use authorized lineage carefully

Only after the brief-native work is complete, inspect the smallest
owner-authorized nearest-sibling evidence. The preferred canonical ledger is
outside packages and client repositories at `~/.design-dna/LEDGER.md`, or an
accountable-owner-approved equivalent. It contains project-safe abstractions,
not a gallery, font history, client assets, screenshots, names, or confidential
copy.

Record one of these honest comparison positions:

- `authorized`: a minimized closest sibling is available and its scope is
  authorized;
- `inherited-system`: repetition is required by an approved brand, product,
  platform, or maintenance system;
- `not-authorized`: no history may be consulted; compare the selected answer
  to the brief-native counter-answer and state the limitation;
- `not-applicable`: no meaningful cross-project comparison exists for the
  project scope.

Never search host directories, infer history, or fabricate a comparator. A
past success is a diagnostic, not a template. Record intentional shared
foundations separately from unexplained carryover.

When a record calls a comparator `closest-sibling`, bind that selection to an
owner-authorized ledger snapshot or an explicit owner-authorized selection
record. The selection record names exactly the comparator IDs marked closest,
why they are nearest for this project, and the authorization boundary. A
relationship label, a remembered previous site, or an unrelated comparison
artifact is not enough to establish closest lineage.

An owner-recurrence requirement is specifically a cross-project claim. Name
the comparator's project identity and select at least one closest sibling from
a different project. A rejected candidate from the current project remains
valuable first-party evidence, but record it as `same-project-rejected`; it
proves whether the revision escaped that candidate, not whether unrelated
studio work has stopped converging. When both risks are active, retain both
comparisons and do not let either stand in for the other.

A different project identifier does not make identical evidence a different
project. Bind each image comparator to its stable source build. The auditor
blocks a closest sibling when a comparator attributed to another project
reuses the exact artifact bytes or exact source-build identity; renaming,
copying, or forking a current-project rejection cannot manufacture
cross-project proof. This provenance check does not judge near matches or
visual similarity, which remain part of the paired human review.

When comparison is authorized but retaining a screenshot would be excessive,
use a hash-bound structural abstract instead: describe the comparator's public
encounter, access basis, purpose, retention/deletion boundary, and what is
lost by not retaining pixels. A structural abstract is not a disguised visual
match or proof of taste. It simply permits a privacy-proportionate human
comparison without importing client media into another project.

## Test the rendered encounter

Build and render the representative route or state before broad propagation.
Bind project-relevant wide and narrow captures to the exact candidate. Each
accepted comparison claim uses the full wide/narrow pair for every route it
names, and complete unprimed, paired, and owner review exposure names the full
declared wide/narrow candidate evidence behind the review. Start
with an unprimed first observation from neutral-label candidate captures only.
Record the reviewer relationship, exact candidate capture IDs, and the
hash-bound review artifact. A self-review is useful diagnostic evidence but
cannot be described as independent. Freeze that unprimed observation before a
paired review exposes candidate captures together with authorized comparator
artifacts; the paired record names both evidence sets and its observation time
is strictly after the unprimed freeze (equal timestamps do not establish an
order). Under owner recurrence, the paired review also names the verified
`closest-sibling` artifact; another authorized comparator cannot replace it.
Owner acceptance then occurs strictly after that paired review is frozen, so
an earlier acceptance cannot be retroactively treated as informed review. The
acceptance record also names the exact candidate build and captured evidence it
reviewed; a general email or project-level approval cannot be retold as an
acceptance of a later render.

Each Project Contrast capture binds its candidate build, route, declared
browser viewport, full-page PNG, and either default or completed allowlisted
interaction state to a named capture ID in a hash-bound schema-3
`render-review.json` package. The auditor verifies the installed renderer's
tool/schema identity, its path-bound output marker, the report build, route,
final URL origin, viewport, state semantics, exact PNG path/hash, and image
dimensions. A local review also retains the renderer's frozen source snapshot
and manifest/digest before it can support contrast evidence; a remote review
retains the renderer's explicit null snapshot policy. Local builds must stay on loopback; a remote review must stay on
the canonical remote origin it declared. Render evidence also has a modest
240-by-240 CSS-pixel viewport floor, matching the shipped schema-3 renderer,
so a one-pixel image or an adapter-only profile cannot pose as a wide or narrow
review. Those facts establish what was rendered, not a preferred device,
breakpoint, style, or aesthetic result. A
full-page image may be taller than the browser viewport even when it captures
an interaction state; state and image extent are separate facts.

This contract does **not** claim a per-capture timestamp, a separately measured
device-pixel ratio, or live browser re-execution. Schema-3 report execution
metadata and declared profile data remain report-level evidence. The binding
also is not a cryptographic signature against someone able to rewrite both an
owned report and its marker; it is an integrity and route/state linkage for
ordinary project evidence, followed by human review.

Ask:

1. What does this visitor encounter first, do next, and reach at the end?
2. What project material makes that sequence appropriate here?
3. After mentally removing subject nouns, dominant media, accent, and motion,
   does the remaining opening, content operation, body progression, spatial
   model, and narrow encounter still belong to this project?
4. Does the visible public shell remain a justified system, or is it the same
   public campaign frame under a different project name?
5. Do the bound surface-grammar observations describe a source-derived public
   language, including any intentionally shared decision, rather than a
   cosmetic reskin?
6. Which shared decisions are justified by brand, task, platform, access, or
   maintenance, and which have no project cause?
7. If it is still too close, which earliest decision must reopen?

Do not solve a failed collision test by rotating surface ingredients. Reopen
the earliest shared decision: visitor occasion, opening model, dominant content
unit, body progression, media role, interaction purpose, or mobile
transformation. Rerender the affected conditions and record the new evidence.

At reviewed owner-recurrence stage, a counterfactual that claims a result must
name at least one removed or swapped project element, and at least one
project-specific observable prediction must be `observed` or `revised`.
An all-`not-applicable` list is honest planning data, but not proof that the
chosen direction was followed through in the rendered scope.

## Use the contract and audit

For an ordinary unlike-recent-work request, initialize Standard plus Project
Contrast with the installed skill root, not by copying unresolved files by
hand:

```text
python -B "<DESIGN_DNA_SKILL_ROOT>/scripts/init_project_state.py" --project "<PROJECT_ROOT>" --profile project-contrast
```

This creates the Project Contrast record and its Standard evidence boundary; it
does not create a Direction Challenge record.

Use the following command only for the explicit three-root recurrence
escalation. It pairs Project Contrast with Direction Challenge and must not be
used merely because the project is a Showcase, portfolio sample, demo, pitch,
or evaluation:

```text
python -B "<DESIGN_DNA_SKILL_ROOT>/scripts/init_project_state.py" --project "<PROJECT_ROOT>" --profile showcase --trigger owner-recurrence-requirement
```

The resulting `.design-dna/project-contrast.json` captures the brief-native
anchor, selected answer, counter-answer, comparison authority, justified
reuse, capture evidence, and human disposition. Its auditor validates record
integrity and evidence binding, not beauty or authorship:

If the paired Project Contrast and Direction Challenge records already exist
for that explicit escalation, add the owner trigger without overwriting either
record's existing evidence:

```text
python -B "<DESIGN_DNA_SKILL_ROOT>/scripts/init_project_state.py" --project "<PROJECT_ROOT>" --add-trigger owner-recurrence-requirement
```

The operation is idempotent. It requires both recurrence records rather than
silently treating a trigger request as a cosmetic setting or creating an
unpaired claim.
The audit asserts readiness by default; use `--allow-incomplete` only when
collecting a clearly provisional diagnostic report.

```text
python -B "<DESIGN_DNA_SKILL_ROOT>/scripts/project_contrast_audit.py" "<PROJECT_ROOT>" --contract ".design-dna/project-contrast.json" --output ".design-dna/project-contrast-audit.json" --require-ready
```

This command's `ready` result is **candidate record readiness**: it verifies
this Project Contrast contract and the exact artifacts it names. It does not
mean owner-approved. A pending or not-requested owner review is reported
separately and does not stop the agent from completing a validated candidate;
an owner rejection or malformed acceptance record still blocks readiness. When
`owner-recurrence-requirement` is active, the release-capability claim also
requires `init_project_state.py --check-ready`, which verifies the paired
Project Contrast/Direction Challenge state, capabilities, and trigger wiring.
Do not treat either standalone auditor as a substitute for that project-state
gate.

The audit may report a malformed renderer package, a wrong route/state/image
binding, missing evidence, an unresolved `still-too-close` disposition, an
unbound comparator, or a cosmetic-only contrast claim. It must never calculate
an AI score, declare a site visually unique, or fail a project because it used
a particular font, palette, shape, layout, image, animation, or component.

For an owner-scoped visual-grammar complaint, ensure the selected qualitative
axes cover the relationships the owner actually named. Container/backplate
logic and action/link/underline affordance are legitimate owner-selected axes,
alongside opening, type posture, body progression, media role, and mobile
transformation. They are not permanent required fields for unrelated briefs.

## Handle related systems and routes

For a site with multiple route jobs, use the lighter route-owned body review
before copying a page recipe. Keep semantic navigation and operating
foundations predictable. Use Range Study when the brief explicitly claims
meaningful expressive range across an anthology or a deliberately varied route
family. Project Contrast does not require every route or client to differ in
every visible dimension.

Declare the public-route evidence boundary in `scope.route_coverage`:

- `representative` for an explicitly representative route or state;
- `all-discovered-public-routes` when every safely discovered static public
  route is in the scope; or
- `sampled-with-rationale` for a documented representative route sample.

The audit discovers routes only from one unambiguous, ordinary static output
root. It intentionally does not guess dynamic router, redirect, query, hash,
or linked routes. Where static discovery is safe, map every discovered public
route to either a direct captured wide/narrow pair or to one directly reviewed
representative route. A representative mapping names why the two routes have
the same job and system boundary; it cannot form a chain or merely say they
look similar. `all-discovered-public-routes` permits only direct captures. A
multi-route static output therefore cannot clear itself with an arbitrary
two-route sample. This is coverage evidence, not a page-count or
visual-difference quota.

For several unrelated projects, Batch Study remains the controlled protocol:
isolate brief/source packets and build contexts, freeze unprimed observations,
then compare neutral-label evidence with authorized data handling. Batch Study
tests evidence coverage and human review protocol; it does not grant an
automatic aesthetic pass.

## Close honestly

A Project Contrast candidate record is ready only when its selected direction has a
project-derived cause, its counter-answer and selection rationale are present,
comparison authority is honest, relevant wide/narrow evidence is bound, and a
hash-bound review with a disclosed relationship has a clear disposition. An
independent agent review may expose a blind spot but must not be described as a
human review or owner acceptance. Owner acceptance is a separate claim. When it
is needed for publication, portfolio use, release, or an explicit owner policy,
an accountable owner or owner-authorized human must record the acceptance
relationship and evidence before that claim is made. Its absence does not force
an autonomous agent to leave an otherwise validated candidate unfinished. A
review file is hash-bound but its prose
is not automatically interpreted by the audit; the structured exposure IDs,
timestamps, and first observation make the review boundary inspectable. A
`still-too-close` result blocks the
Project Contrast claim until the design is reopened and rerendered; it does not
prove a technical, accessibility, owner-acceptance, or production failure.

The goal is a defensible difference in how unrelated projects are conceived
and encountered relative to the authorized evidence. It cannot prove human
authorship, category exclusivity, universal uniqueness, originality in all
possible references, or universal aesthetic approval.
