# Enterprise Candidate

Use this for every fresh public website unless the task is an explicitly
bounded repair or non-public surface. It makes routine polish and verification
autonomous; it does not give permission to invent business facts, claim a
financial valuation, turn every client into a global corporation, or force a
single visual style.

The target is a public candidate that a visitor could plausibly encounter from
an established organization in the stated category, at the truthful scale the
brief supports. A concise local-service site, concept site, publication, or
campaign may meet this bar differently from a global retailer. The standard is
the quality of the public encounter, not the size of the fictional organization.

## Before direction: establish the public reality

Start with the minimum truthful model that changes design decisions:

- who arrives, what they need to understand, and the next useful move;
- which ordinary public questions the category creates and which content,
  route, or state answers each one;
- which promised controls need an actual outcome, an honest unavailable state,
  or removal;
- what media is needed for recognition, evidence, atmosphere, comparison, or
  orientation; and
- what would make the result read as a lightweight demo, generic first draft,
  or an implausible public surface.

Do not manufacture departments, locations, people, statistics, press, case
studies, testimonials, inventory, policy claims, accounts, or integrations to
imitate enterprise scale. A concept can be explicit about its boundary while
still offering a coherent and convincingly designed visitor journey.

Read [Public copy integrity](public-copy-integrity.md) before drafting
prominent headlines, subheads, brand statements, and actions. A strong visual
direction does not justify unsupported manifesto copy, automatic “X reasons”
framing, or decorative `01 / 02 / 03` rhythm.

Read [Reference-led direction](reference-led-direction.md) before choosing the
first public visual candidate. Complete the project-local `reference-dossier.md`
with the exact audience/task/content/brand/operational selection brief; a
side-by-side candidate comparison and concrete rejection reasons; at least six
strong references from at least three sources; distinct full-page wide and
narrow captures plus truthful motion/static evidence for each strong row; at
least three brief-specific counterexamples; and a selected synthesis of at
least four references. A publicly visible gallery entry is eligible only for
what it exposes; a restricted entry is not. One selected source supplies the
dominant grammar for each route, compatible mapped sources supply supporting
moments, and no visible part comes from producer taste.

Initialize the evidence lane before broad implementation. For an ordinary new
public build, use the Enterprise Candidate profile:

```text
python -B "<DESIGN_DNA_SKILL_ROOT>/scripts/init_project_state.py" --project "<PROJECT_ROOT>" --profile enterprise-candidate
```

When another selected profile already creates the `direction` and
`visual-review` records, retain that profile and add
`--evidence-capability enterprise-candidate` rather than discarding the
separate obligation. For example, an owner-required Direction Challenge uses
its own profile plus this evidence capability. This composes review rigor; it
does not prescribe visual style.

Before a second section or route is implemented, render the primary first
screen at the manifest's wide and narrow viewports and run `gate.py --phase
first-screen --route-key <PRIMARY_KEY>` against the full planned route
manifest. Bind the passing `.design-dna/evidence/first-screen-gate.json` as
`First-screen gate` in the direction proof. A missing or failing record is a
prebuild hard stop; the final gate cannot substitute for it later.

## Plan the composition and media before the route family spreads

For every opening or high-attention media treatment, name the media role and
the relationship between the intended slot and the actual source:

- source aspect and the target wide/narrow slot;
- subject-safe bounds, including faces, bodies, products, objects, or evidence
  that cannot be clipped accidentally;
- whether the image should crop, remain whole, be replaced, be reframed, or
  cause a different composition; and
- the content consequence of any overlay, empty area, background field, or
  text placement.

An intrinsic portrait image should not quietly set a massive grid row. A fixed
landscape slot should not silently crop the only person or product out of a
scene. A full-scene treatment should not create a dead white panel merely to
avoid the crop. Solve the actual relationship with source selection, framing,
or a different composition before scaling the pattern across routes.

## Build mature public behavior, not visual theater

Give each route and section one real job. The system may use cards, panels,
rules, imagery, density, hierarchy, or a strong visual idea when each supports
a visitor-facing object, comparison, state, action, context, or identity. Do
not substitute branded scenery for usable category depth.

Review the primary visitor paths promised by the site. Navigation, discovery,
filters, product/service selection, forms, commerce, support, content detail,
and error/recovery states apply only when the project offers them. Implement
them fully, bound them honestly, or remove them. Do not let a polished homepage
hide dead controls or thin downstream routes.

Maintain a coherent system, but let different route and content jobs receive
different rhythms. A shared brand should not turn every opening, action,
backplate, card, crop, or mobile collapse into the same repeated maneuver.

## Enterprise Candidate review before preview

Inspect exact wide and narrow rendered captures before presenting the work as
ready. Review the whole public candidate without the rationale first, then use
the following prompts to name observed causes:

1. **Public maturity.** Would this make sense as a finished public surface for
   this category and truthful scale? What ordinary visitor question remains
   unanswered or obscured?
2. **First screen.** Is the primary subject, heading, and useful next move
   visible and deliberate? Is there an accidental void, clipped media, title
   outside the opening, unsupported decorative panel, or misleading hero?
3. **Media credibility.** Does each high-attention image have the right role,
   source, framing, and responsive behavior? Does it look physically and
   commercially plausible for the public proposition?
4. **System finish.** Do type, copy, controls, spacing, information grouping,
   and visual intensity preserve hierarchy and support routine use as well as
   expressive moments?
5. **State and route-family finish.** Do direct entry, navigation, key actions,
   compact widths, and materially different route bodies work without exposing
   a first draft, a dead end, or a repeated component failure?
6. **Public copy integrity.** Does the complete public corpus have a source-led
   copy map? Does every high-attention line have a public job and an approved
   voice or project anchor? Does each rendered paragraph-like block carry one
   clear thought rather than fill space with generic brand theater? Remove or
   recompose the writing model instead of rewriting around it.
7. **Numeric rhetoric.** Does every count, ordinal, or step label convey a
   factual quantity, real comparison, or decision-relevant sequence? Remove
   invented “X reasons,” “Y steps,” and decorative counters rather than merely
   changing `01` to `1` or spelling the number out.
8. **Reference-led direction.** Is the completed dossier built from eligible
   public evidence selected for this exact audience, task, content model,
   brand, and operating reality rather than accolade or convenience? Were
   serious candidates studied across complete accessible pages, states, and
   wide/narrow conditions, with selection and rejection reasons recorded? Do
   at least six references from at least three sources, and at least three
   observed counterexamples, support one coherent dominant grammar per route
   without a generic template, visual collage, copied brand surface, or
   producer-designed connective tissue?

Record the disposition in the Enterprise Candidate section of
`visual-review.md`. A producer may perform this review, but label it honestly
as self-review; it is not owner acceptance or independent validation.

## Repair shared causes, not screenshots

When a material flaw appears, identify the lowest shared decision that produced
it: information model, route job, media/source, composition, component rule,
responsive rule, copy model, or interaction state. Repair that decision, then
rerender the affected route family at wide and narrow widths. Do not fix one
captured page while leaving its siblings, mobile transformation, or source rule
broken.

Routine iteration is autonomous. Pause only for a genuine missing authority:
facts, brand direction, cultural authority, rights, material scope, delivery,
or a choice whose answer would materially change the public candidate.

## What can be claimed

State the verified dimensions of the result, such as rendered route coverage,
responsive review, implemented states, or known delivery boundaries. Do not say
the site is objectively "perfect," "trillion-dollar," undetectably AI-made, or
accepted by the owner unless that fact is independently true. A passing
Enterprise Candidate closure means the named public-surface checks were
performed and material observed defects were resolved or honestly disclosed.
