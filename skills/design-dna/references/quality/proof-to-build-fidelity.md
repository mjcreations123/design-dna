# Proof-to-build fidelity

Use this after a direction proof is selected, when implementing a supplied
design, or when a local change risks erasing accepted visual character.

Fidelity means preserving accepted decisions and intent while authoring the
states and adaptations the proof could not show. It does not mean copying
pixels into every condition or forcing the selected proof into a fixed system.

## Bind what the proof actually establishes

A screenshot or canvas does not prove DOM structure, content rules,
responsive behavior, component identity, interactions, accessibility, or
runtime assets. Bind the proof to its candidate/build, source or content
packet, media inventory, route/state, viewport, date, and artifact hash at the
assurance level the project requires. Mark behavior inferred from pixels as
unknown.

When learning from an authorized reference, record what may be transferred and
what identity, copy, asset, composition, or proprietary behavior must not be
copied.

## Extract an extensible decision model

Carry forward the selected candidate's `creative_logic` and consequential
observable decisions. Do not translate the proof into mandatory categories it
never used.

| Decision ID | Proof evidence | Accepted decision | What should remain observable | Scope | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | route/state/viewport and region |  | render, task, state, or audience response | systemic, route-local, component-local, one-off, or exception |  |

The ledger may include content, composition, typography, media, color,
material, ornament, interaction, motion, behavior, or any project-specific
concern. Add only what is consequential.

Distinguish:

- constants required for recognition, comprehension, behavior, or approved
  character;
- variables expected to adapt by route, content, state, viewport, input,
  language, preference, campaign, or platform;
- local decisions and intentional one-offs;
- unknowns the proof did not settle;
- exceptions whose optical or experiential purpose would be lost through
  premature abstraction.

Reuse genuine relationships and reliable behavior. Do not componentize away
the selected logic, and do not preserve superficial similarity when the real
content or task requires another form.

## Bind decisions to implementation

Use the proof-to-build delta ledger. For each accepted decision, record:

- source/content/asset/token/component authority;
- implementation location and current state;
- relevant content, size, input, language, preference, loading, and failure
  behavior;
- permitted deviation and reason;
- current rendered or executable evidence;
- disposition and accountable owner.

Maintain state coverage for the conditions the product can actually enter.
Typical states may include loading, empty, error, success, disabled, selected,
long or translated content, input methods, themes, and motion preferences, but
there is no required universal matrix. Omit irrelevant combinations with a
reason. Missing relevant behavior is not a passing state.

## Steer locally

For a requested change:

1. bind the route, state, viewport, and exact region;
2. identify the decision or hard constraint involved;
3. protect accepted work outside that scope;
4. change the smallest coherent cause;
5. inspect source and rendered behavior in the same conditions;
6. run whole-page and shared-consumer regression where the change can spread.

Regenerate broadly only when the creative logic itself has been reopened. Do
not preserve pixel parity when it breaks truth, real content, responsive
behavior, accessibility, or function; record the justified adaptation.

## Reconcile the delta

Before calling the build faithful, verify that:

- every accepted consequential decision is implemented, intentionally
  adapted, rejected, pending, or blocked with an owner;
- the selected `creative_logic` remains recognizable in the exact conditions
  where it was meant to operate;
- real content, media dimensions, states, and interactions replace proof-only
  assumptions where available;
- relevant responsive, input, language, accessibility, preference, loading,
  and failure behavior is confirmed rather than inferred;
- visible differences from the selected proof have human or accountable-owner
  dispositions at the assurance level required;
- the approved identity was not silently replaced by a producer default.

Do not require a named aesthetic device, route archetype count, or
standardized extraction taxonomy unless the selected candidate or project
authority actually declared it.

Never auto-accept a changed visual baseline. Record browser, viewport, pixel
density when material, fonts, locale, theme, motion condition, data fixture,
masks, and known exclusions so later comparison refers to the same evidence.
