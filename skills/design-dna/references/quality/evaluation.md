## Separate review lenses

Keep perception, direction parity, specificity, implementation, specialist,
and target-user evidence distinguishable. Use the lenses that apply; do not
turn them into one undifferentiated score.

- **Perception:** use screenshots, recordings, or the live artifact for
  project fit, hierarchy, composition, visual quality, currentness,
  credibility, and audience response.
- **Direction parity:** compare the final candidate with the selected
  `creative_logic`, observable decisions, proof, and previous accepted
  baseline; separate intentional evolution from drift.
- **Specificity closure:** when the request explicitly rejects generic,
  templated, repeated, vibe-coded, or allegedly AI-looking work, run the
  [adversarial specificity review](specificity-review.md) after an unprimed
  perception pass.
- **Implementation:** exercise live behavior, semantics, content, states,
  responsiveness, accessibility, performance, and engineering quality.
- **Specialist or target-user evidence:** preserve its distinct authority,
  method, sample, and limitations.

A screenshot cannot prove keyboard behavior or truth. A test suite cannot
prove visual hierarchy or owner acceptance.

For a substantial greenfield public surface, visual redesign, or
owner-rejected candidate, seek an unbriefed perception review from someone
other than the builder when an independent reviewer or accountable owner is
available. Show the artifact before its rationale or diagnostic vocabulary.
Self-review can find defects but cannot be relabeled as independent or owner
evidence. If none is available, record `independent perception not performed`.

## Build the relevant matrix

Select routes, tasks, states, content, viewports, containers, inputs,
languages, preferences, data, browsers, and resource conditions that can
materially affect the outcome. Include production-like information without
exposing private data.

Typical conditions include narrow through wide and short-height layouts;
touch, keyboard, pointer, hover/no-hover; default, loading, empty, error,
success, disabled, permission, destructive, cancellation, and recovery;
content extremes; translation and RTL; theme, zoom, text enlargement, reduced
motion, and contrast preferences. This is an extensible condition set, not a
universal screenshot quota. Mark relevant omissions and why.

## Choose project questions

Use the questions that can decide this project's readiness:

- **Project fit:** Which real project, audience, owner, cultural, or system
  evidence explains the result?
- **Creative logic:** Are the accepted observable decisions present and well
  executed? Does the candidate's singular, plural, layered, familiar,
  decorative, restrained, maximal, or other logic work on its own terms?
- **Visual quality:** Do typography, composition, spacing, color
  relationships, media, material, ornament, interaction, and detail reach the
  brief's quality bar where they apply?
- **Audience response:** Do attention, emotion, comprehension, invitation,
  recall, and use behave as intended without forcing a fixed sequence or
  universal score?
- **Information and route structure:** Do content and interactions follow real
  questions and tasks? Is shared structure justified rather than repeated by
  habit?
- **Copy and claims:** Are voice, labels, actions, proof, numbers, and endings
  concrete, authoritative, and free of an unexplained rhetorical machine?
- **Aesthetic autonomy:** Do ornamental, atmospheric, playful, or formal
  choices succeed in the whole composition without pretending to be evidence
  or utility?
- **Credibility:** Are claims, assets, controls, metadata, generated status,
  placeholders, and operating states truthful?
- **Usability and resilience:** Can people complete, understand, continue,
  recover, and adapt across applicable content, size, input, language,
  network, and preference conditions?
- **Cognitive and accessibility fit:** Do status, recognition, consequences,
  help, semantics, focus, contrast, alternatives, and recovery serve the real
  audience and task?
- **Care:** Do ordinary copy, actions, states, media, accessibility,
  responsive behavior, performance, metadata, and failure details show that
  the team understood the actual subject?
- **Creative freedom:** Is a choice being rewarded or rejected merely because
  it appeared in a trend or warning list? Judge the rendered relationship and
  credible counterevidence instead.
- **Typography:** Do actual fonts, roles, typesetting, spacing, wrapping,
  scripts, fallbacks, loading, and tone make a convincing and accessible
  contribution for the real copy without family-name policing?

Add project-specific questions. Omit irrelevant ones with an honest
disposition when a durable record requires it. Do not convert the list into an
AI-authorship score or a fixed aesthetic recipe.

## Bind the evidence

For each consequential capture, recording, or executable result, record what
is needed to reproduce and interpret it:

- route, task, state, content/data, and relevant environment;
- viewport or container, zoom, input, browser/version, locale, theme, and
  preference conditions;
- candidate/build identifier, source/content/media identity, date, artifact
  path, and hash where the assurance level requires them;
- reviewer relationship, independence, prior exposure, and review lens;
- observed result, cause, counterevidence, impact, fix, rerun, limitation,
  status, and owner.

Use the shared [human-review severity rubric](review-severity.md). Keep impact,
diagnostic confidence, and personal taste separate. Self-review is not
independent perception evidence. Do not calculate an AI probability or declare
authorship from aesthetic signals.
