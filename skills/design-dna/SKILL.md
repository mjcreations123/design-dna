# Design DNA

Create web work whose content, hierarchy, system, and behavior are visibly
chosen for this project and audience. Optimize for specificity, clarity,
credibility, usability, time-register fit, and finish. Diagnose observable
design and implementation causes; never promise undetectable AI involvement,
score aesthetic authorship, or claim human-only creation.

## Resolve authority

Use this order:

1. Safety, law, privacy, accessibility, factual integrity, and repository instructions.
2. Explicit user, accountable owner, approved client, product, and brand requirements.
3. The task, surface mode, content, stack, and delivery constraints.
4. Project evidence and documented rationale.
5. A project-local accountable-owner policy when supplied, otherwise the
   bundled [publisher defaults](policy/owner-defaults.yml), then heuristics.

When same-tier sources conflict, preserve an established approved requirement,
identify the accountable decision owner, and ask only when the unresolved
choice would materially change the result. Owner defaults guide judgment; they
are not project approval.

## Classify the work

| Scope | Required process |
| --- | --- |
| New build, visual redesign, or route family | Preflight, establish direction, proof, implement, and complete rendered plus engineering review. |
| Component or meaningful visual change | Inherit the system, define the component's job and states, and test changed behavior and containers. |
| Visual or UX review | Inspect available rendered and source evidence; report observed causes and unperformed checks. |
| Mechanical or purely functional change | Preserve the visual system and verify proportionately. |

Choose a scope and all applicable capabilities. Quick and Standard are
alternative proportional base requests; Showcase, Range Study, High-risk, and
Asset-led add their own requirements when triggered:

| Capability preset | Use when | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quick | A bounded repair or established-system change has low design and operational risk. | Inspect context, preserve the system, implement changed states, and run affected checks. |
| Standard | A new route, meaningful feature, or ordinary redesign establishes or extends direction. | Frame, direct, prove consequential decisions proportionately, implement, and complete rendered plus engineering review. |
| Showcase | The work is expressive, premium, highly visible, owner-sensitive, or explicitly rejects safe or generic output. | Research the decision, compare enough rendered evidence to challenge the first default, deepen risk-selected proof, polish, run adversarial review, and keep owner acceptance separate. |
| Range Study | A multi-route brief explicitly asks pages to demonstrate meaningful creative range. | Keep dependable truth, navigation, accessibility, identity, and operations; author the route-family record; prove routes selected by uncertainty; verify real paths; and review a matched route atlas. |
| High-risk | The surface handles consequential transactions, identity, permissions, private data, money, regulated claims, or difficult recovery. | Prioritize task, state, content, specialist, and real-user evidence; visual ambition cannot waive a safety or production gate. |
| Asset-led | Material imagery, video, audio, fonts, documents, maps, embeds, or generated media needs a durable record. | Gate every listed asset on type-specific provenance, rights, privacy, factual status, approval, delivery, accessibility, and generated-media evidence. |

The persisted capability set is cumulative. A Showcase can also be a Range
Study and High-risk, and recorded assets add an Asset-led gate; adding one
cannot silently remove another. Redundant Quick or Standard tiers are
normalized away.

Identify every surface mode that materially helps describe the user's job:

- Decide or convert: [Persuade](references/modes/persuade.md).
- Explore or experience a sequence: [Experience](references/modes/experience.md).
- Complete a workflow: [Operate](references/modes/operate.md).
- Comprehend or reference: [Read](references/modes/read.md).

Do not force a hybrid route into one primary mode. A route may persuade,
support exploration, enable operation, and sustain reading in the same
experience; use only the guidance that answers its actual decisions. For four
contrasting examples of evidence becoming design without a reusable visual
recipe, read [decision case studies](references/decision-case-studies.md).

## Preserve creative freedom

Keep low-freedom rules for truth, rights, privacy, safety, accessibility,
working behavior, repository constraints, and explicit owner or brand
requirements. Keep aesthetic decisions high-freedom. Design DNA has no global
font, palette, style, geometry, ornament, component, layout, interaction,
motion, section-order, energy-arc, media-count, or concept-count whitelist or
blacklist. Common choices are not automatically generic; uncommon choices are
not automatically good.

A fitting direction may be singular, layered, plural, restrained, maximal,
decorative, conventional, historically referential, experimental, or
deliberately dissonant. Coherence means the decisions make sense for this
project; it does not require one idea, one contrast, or one visual device to
govern everything. Treat examples and diagnostic patterns as lenses, never as
menus or inverse prompts. Read [creative freedom](references/creative-freedom.md)
before imposing an aesthetic rule that did not come from the project.

## Keep these invariants

- Preserve repository instructions, unrelated work, established systems, and working integrations unless the task authorizes change.
- Do not invent business facts, proof, people, metrics, reviews, supplied or
  documentary assets, availability, product evidence, or integrations.
  User-authorized decorative or concept media is allowed only within the
  user's project authority and under the
  generated-media, provenance, disclosure, rights, privacy, and factual
  boundaries in the imagery and asset-integrity guidance.
- Do not activate publishing, payments, ordering, booking, form delivery, tracking, accounts, maps, or other external behavior without authority and real configuration.
- Use familiar patterns when they serve the task. Create specificity from project evidence and coherent craft, not random novelty, staged roughness, or a replacement house style.
- For a Range Study, count only independently addressable routes and keep
  repeated navigation semantics predictable. Judge route difference from the
  rendered bodies and their actual jobs, not from a required checklist of
  visual dimensions. Palette, copy, or photograph replacement alone does not
  prove material difference; neither must every route change its font, motion,
  or media model.
- Give every substantial greenfield public surface enough resolved character
  that an unbriefed reviewer can connect it to this project rather than only a
  starter pattern. The source may be content, convention, material, crop,
  proportion, typography, rhythm, ornament, interaction, restraint, or another
  fitting choice. No particular channel or combination is mandatory.
- Do not scale a consequential open direction before its important aesthetic
  uncertainty has been rendered. Explore enough materially different evidence
  to challenge the first plausible answer; the useful amount may be one
  refined reference-bound proof, several rough provocations, or parallel
  routes. Scale exploration with uncertainty and stakes, record what changed
  the decision, and never claim an unrendered comparison occurred.
- Match visible ambition to the brief. Avoiding generic patterns is not a
  request for timidity, emptiness, or default minimalism. Expressive work may
  derive its impact from one governing idea, many local logics, atmosphere,
  accumulation, humor, ornament, strong convention, deliberate collision, or
  another project-fitting approach. Review the rendered effect; do not require
  a signature device, fixed number of channels, bounded risk, or loud/quiet
  pair.
- Make public orientation appropriate to the experience without relying on a
  designer's explanation. For service, product, and task-led work, a new
  visitor should be able to identify what is offered, the relevant audience or
  situation, and a useful action. Art, narrative, editorial, and entertainment
  work may deliberately delay fuller explanation when the invitation,
  orientation, and continuation remain intentional and usable.
- Choose a deliberate media strategy for work whose value is physical,
  sensory, spatial, product-led, hospitality-led, retail-led, food-led, or
  event-led. Missing supplied photography is an input gap, not an automatic
  instruction to make a photo-free site. When the owner authorizes generated
  concept media and the capability exists, art-direct, inspect, record, and
  use it within the imagery and asset-integrity boundaries.
- Keep customer-facing copy about the customer's subject, decision, and next
  step. Do not turn design rationale, prompt logic, evidence gaps, unresolved
  inputs, or repeated concept disclaimers into the public content system.
- Judge typography with real copy in its actual role. Verify reading comfort,
  display legibility, hierarchy, wrap, script coverage, loading, and relevant
  widths without imposing a preferred family, pairing, spacing formula, or
  degree of compression.
- Complete an adversarial specificity review against the final implementation
  for every substantial new build or visual redesign; a scanner or first
  render is not closure. When the request explicitly rejects an AI-looking,
  vibe-coded, templated, generic, or repeated result, use the review's deeper
  cross-route, copy, media, and comparison lenses.
- Review repeated isolated-word color, gradient, face, italic, underline,
  marker, or sticker treatments in prominent copy when they appear automatic,
  interchangeable, or unsupported by the rendered composition. A single
  treatment is not a defect by ingredient. Brand, editorial, expressive,
  semantic, and purely compositional uses may all be valid; preserve meaning
  without color alone, contrast, legibility, reflow, and forced-colors
  behavior.
- Preserve semantic structure, keyboard and touch access, visible focus, contrast, responsive reflow, reduced motion, content variation, and resilient fallbacks.
- Implement visible controls and relevant states, or remove, disable with explanation, or clearly defer them.
- Never claim a browser, screenshot, user, accessibility, performance, security, host, build, or release check occurred when it did not.
- When a community, religion, language, ethnicity, or another lived identity
  is materially central, record its authority, terminology, representation,
  and cultural-review boundary. The producing agent cannot self-certify
  cultural acceptance or owner-authorize its absence.
- Direct rejection from the accountable owner or observed target-user
  confusion reopens the affected work. It outranks a clean source scan,
  passing automated suite, prior author self-review, or unlabeled aesthetic
  confidence until the revised result is rendered and reviewed again.

## Follow the process spine

For a substantial build or redesign, read [the workflow](references/workflow.md)
and use it as the process spine:

1. Inspect the project and frame the audience, task, facts, assets, constraints, and delivery state.
2. Gather enough truthful source material for the claims and decisions, define
   the public orientation or entry condition where it matters, establish the
   content and route structure, and choose the media path. For a culturally
   central subject, name the accountable owner, represented context, and review
   authority before visual commitment.
3. When direction is open or ambition is high, decompose a current,
   retrieval-dated reference dossier, render enough project-specific evidence
   to expose the important choice, record perception before diagnostic
   vocabulary, select with rationale, and preserve a reversible checkpoint.
4. Proof the selected direction's consequential decisions with real copy and
   relevant viewports. Test typography comfort, public comprehension, and fit
   to the requested ambition without requiring every project to prove the same
   aesthetic categories.
5. Extract only the reusable rules the project actually needs; keep justified
   one-offs without forcing them into a component system. For a Range Study,
   separate shared foundations from route bodies, then proof the routes with
   the highest uncertainty and the most useful contrast.
6. Lock representative evidence sufficient to prevent expensive propagation
   errors, then implement the real path and states within the existing
   technical contract. For a Range Study, record the route family before
   scaling, but choose proof routes from project risk rather than fixed
   archetypes.
7. Render, inspect, revise causes, rerun affected checks, obtain accountable
   human acceptance when the owner requires it, and disclose remaining limits.

Ask only high-leverage concept questions whose answers would materially change
direction. Ask additional focused questions for factual, risky, externally
acting, or irreversible choices when the answer is required.

## Coordinate specialists

Design DNA owns project-specific art direction, content hierarchy, visual
coherence, responsive composition, and rendered finish. A specialist skill
owns its narrower domain contract—for example deep accessibility, security,
performance, SEO, 3D, data, legal, backend, or deployment. Repository and user
instructions still outrank both. Combine conclusions at the boundary instead
of letting a specialist introduce an unrelated visual system or letting visual
polish waive a specialist gate. When guidance conflicts, satisfy the stricter
safety, truth, access, and operational requirement, then preserve the chosen
direction within that constraint.

## Bound readiness and evidence

"Production-quality design and implementation" describes only verified
dimensions. Authentication, personal data, payments, uploads, user-generated
content, regulated claims, public indexing, external integrations, and
production operations require the applicable specialist review or an explicit
`unverified` release block.

Create only useful evidence records with `scripts/init_project_state.py`. Direct
evidence templates are [creative exploration](templates/exploration-template.md),
[direction](templates/direction-template.md),

[proof-to-build delta](templates/proof-build-delta-template.md),
[compiled design-context capsule](templates/design-context-capsule-template.md),

[framework-neutral state-matrix example](templates/state-matrix.example.yml),
[service blueprint](templates/service-blueprint-template.md),

[user validation](templates/user-validation-template.md),
[production handoff](templates/handoff-template.md), and
[scan allowlist](templates/scan-allowlist.json). The
[owner-policy example](templates/owner-policy.example.yml) is a separate,
opt-in governance file rather than an initialized evidence record; follow
[owner-policy onboarding](references/owner-policy.md) and never activate its
placeholders unchanged. Keep mutable records
project-local and apply their stated privacy classification. Before claiming
the listed project records satisfy the selected cumulative assurance
capabilities, run the initializer's `--check-ready` gate; `--check-state` alone
is structural and may legitimately return success while drafts remain.
`--check-ready` covers every record listed in schema-2 `state.json`, including
standalone `--record` additions. It does not infer unlisted records or prove
whole-project, specialist, production, or launch readiness.

If Python 3.10+ or another required capability is unavailable, do not install
or improvise it. Perform the safe manual equivalent, name the checks not run,
and do not broaden the result. The optional scanner supplies bounded
source-review prompts; use [engineering verification](references/quality/engineering-verification.md)
for its complete contract. Private cross-project comparison is opt-in,
owner-authorized, minimized, and free of confidential client material.

Read [evidence policy](references/evidence.md) only when changing a risk rule
or evaluating a new current-pattern claim.
