# Service blueprint

Use this template only when a user's outcome crosses channels, teams, systems,
providers, policies, or backstage operations. Label assumptions and unknowns;
a blueprint does not prove that an operational promise works.

## Scope

- User and scenario:
- Outcome the service must enable:
- Journey start and end:
- Channels and organizations in scope:
- Current, proposed, or mixed service:
- Blueprint owner:
- Version/build and review date:
- Research, service records, analytics, or operational evidence used:
- Material audience, channel, or condition not represented:

## Evidence status

Use one status consistently:

- `verified`: supported by current authoritative evidence;
- `observed`: witnessed in research or operation but not established broadly;
- `reported`: supplied by a named accountable source;
- `hypothesis`: a proposition to test;
- `unknown`: not yet established.

Do not silently promote reported, hypothetical, or unknown service behavior to
verified.

Record only service levels and timing that an accountable source can confirm.
Separate submission, receipt, queueing, processing, third-party waiting, and
outcome when those states differ.
