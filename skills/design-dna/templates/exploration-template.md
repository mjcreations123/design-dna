# Creative exploration

<!-- proportional-evidence-v1 -->

Explore only while a consequential choice remains open. There is no required
number of concepts, axes, artifacts, page forms, fonts, palettes, or devices.
The evidence may be prose, sketches, code, renders, references, or another form
that lets the actual decision-maker compare materially different answers.

## Decision and limits

Record what changed through comparison, why alternatives lost in this context,
the selected candidate or open decision, remaining uncertainty, accountable
owner, and reversible next step. Do not turn one project's rejection into a
global ban.
