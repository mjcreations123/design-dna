# Commercial readiness gates

Price is a market decision, not a software test. This checklist defines the
minimum evidence needed before Design DNA is represented as a premium product.

## Product

- one clear outcome and scope;
- concise buyer quick start;
- one supported installation route per host;
- deterministic install, update, doctor, rollback, and removal;
- versioned migration;
- no hidden network service, tracking, or secret requirement;
- complete license, support, security, privacy, and compatibility terms in the
  sales agreement;
- tested private security and support contact routes with named owners;
- buyer-specific owner-policy onboarding or an explicit publisher-guidance
  boundary;

## Quality

- development audit passes;
- strict release audit passes without relabeling missing evidence;
- zero unresolved critical or high findings;
- every accepted medium or low limitation has an owner, rationale, reach,
  expiry or review date, and mitigation;
- supported operating systems and host/model combinations have current results;
- independent rendered reviewers agree that the skill improves the supported
  dimensions without imposing a recognizable replacement house style.

## Buyer experience

- clean-machine installation has been observed;
- first-use prompts and expected outputs are clear;
- error messages identify the exact remedy;
- update and rollback have been exercised;
- support boundaries and response commitments match the purchase agreement;
- examples use truthful licensed material and remain clearly separate from
  release evidence.

## Claims

Allowed claims must be traceable to current results. Do not claim guaranteed
human authorship, undetectable AI involvement, universal aesthetic superiority,
security certification, legal compliance, or buyer satisfaction.

Strong defensible claims describe the process and measured outcome: grounded
project direction, truth-preserving content, coherent implementation, bounded
source checks, rendered adversarial review, and demonstrated improvement on the
published evaluation set.

## Transaction readiness

Before accepting payment, obtain legal and tax review appropriate to the seller
and buyer, use a signed transaction-specific license, identify the exact
delivered version, and preserve the invoice, agreement, checksum, support term,
and acceptance evidence.

The repository currently contains no tested support/security intake address and
no transaction-specific legal, tax, privacy, or service terms. Those omissions
must remain visible blockers; package validation cannot complete them.
