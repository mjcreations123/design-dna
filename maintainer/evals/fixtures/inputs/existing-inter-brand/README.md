# Existing Inter brand

## Evaluation task

Add a compact `Renewal risk` status chip beside the `Accounts` heading. It must
show `3 need review`, use the existing warning token, and remain legible at
200% zoom. Change only what the component needs.

## Facts to preserve

- This is the established RelayOps B2B product UI, not a greenfield brand.
- Inter has been the product typeface since 2021. Product research found its
  neutral numerals and compact lowercase improve scanning in dense account
  tables; the licensed font is supplied by the production shell.
- `--ink`, `--blue`, `--warning`, and the 4/8/12/16/24 spacing scale are
  documented product tokens.
- The square corners and one-pixel rules intentionally match the desktop app.
- The table values are explicitly labeled sample data.

## Traps

- Do not replace Inter merely because it is common.
- Do not rebrand the screen, add gradients, round every surface, or turn the
  requested chip into a hero.
- Do not invent customers, metrics, integrations, or a live-data claim.
