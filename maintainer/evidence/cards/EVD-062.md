# Qualification needs repeated trials and outcome evidence

## Claim

One passing generation cannot establish reliable skill behavior; qualification
needs repeated trials, first-try and all-trials views, balanced cases, and
inspection of actual outcomes.

## Observation

Anthropic's agent-eval guidance separates tasks, trials, graders, traces, and
outcomes, explains pass@k versus pass^k, and recommends multiple trials,
reference solutions, balanced positive and negative cases, and clear graders.

## Scope and limitations

The guidance targets agents broadly. Visual quality still needs situated human
review, and empirical repeated-run rates should not be presented as theoretical
probabilities without the required assumptions.

## Counterexamples

A deterministic mechanical validator may need only one run after its own unit
tests. An exploratory ideation tool may care about one useful option, while a
client-facing website builder needs consistent first-pass quality.

## Positive action

Report pass@1, empirical all-trials-pass, worst trial, and blocker rate by task
and prompt family; preserve traces and artifacts; distinguish capability
discovery from customer-facing reliability.

## Validation

A qualification result cannot pass from one run, an empty result directory, or
a summary that hides the weakest trial or unresolved blocker.

recommendations and terminology may evolve.
