# Productive friction should challenge the root, not add decorative ceremony

## Claim

Brief-native alternatives, counter-proofs, and deliberate revision checkpoints
can interrupt default convergence without imposing a new universal style.

## Observation

The 2026 web-vibe-coding paper identifies homogenization risks across the
creation lifecycle and proposes productive friction as a mitigation against
the narrowing effects of frictionless generation.

## Scope and limitations

This preprint presents a sociotechnical analysis and framework, not a validated
visual recipe or proof that every familiar interface is harmful.

## Counterexamples

Established patterns can be exactly right for a familiar, high-stakes task.
Friction that exists only to slow work, multiply documents, or force novelty
does not improve design.

## Positive action

Challenge the first safe organizing answer with a materially different,
project-grounded root; render the smallest proof that can settle the decision;
then scale only the selected answer and preserve task-appropriate familiarity.
