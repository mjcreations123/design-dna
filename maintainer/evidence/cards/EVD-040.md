# A coherent system can contain purpose-built local systems

## Claim

A shared foundation need not force one monolithic page recipe; bounded local
systems can serve distinct audiences and uses while remaining connected.

## Observation

Spotify describes Encore as a family or system of systems. Shared foundations
and tokens form the minimum common layer, while local systems hold elements
tailored to particular products, platforms, or audiences.

## Scope and limitations

This is first-party organizational practice from a large product company, not
comparative outcome research. A ten-page editorial site does not need
Spotify's teams, tooling, governance, naming, or visual language.

## Counterexamples

A small conventional site may need one compact system. Creating a local system
for every minor difference can increase inconsistency, ownership ambiguity,
and maintenance cost.

## Positive action

Define the smallest shared identity and accessibility spine, then locate
route-specific compositions in a bounded local layer with an explicit purpose,
owner, and reuse expectation.

## Supports

`RISK-SYS-001`, `RISK-REPEAT-001`

## Validation

Inventory shared foundations and local route systems, confirm that every local
departure answers a route need, and check that the shared layer has not
silently expanded into an identical body template.

## Retention

Public first-party design practice; retain a dated summary and review within
90 days. Do not copy Spotify's proprietary system or claim its outcomes.
