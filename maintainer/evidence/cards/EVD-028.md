# Synthetic-media disclosure requires an applicability review

## Claim

Public generated or manipulated media can trigger jurisdiction-, role-, and
content-specific transparency duties; a generic design checklist cannot decide
applicability.

## Observation

The European Commission published guidelines for Article 50 transparency
obligations in July 2026 and distinguishes duties by provider/deployer role and
the type and use of generated or manipulated content.

## Scope and limitations

The guidance concerns the EU AI Act and is not universal legal advice. Effective
dates, exceptions, roles, and later law or guidance can change the result.

## Counterexamples

Some generated design material will fall outside a particular duty, while
owners may choose additional voluntary disclosure for trust or policy reasons.

## Positive action

Record jurisdiction, role, transformation, provenance, disclosure basis, and
legal-review status; obtain qualified review rather than applying a blanket
label or omitting disclosure by default.

## Validation

Review the final asset, use context, transformation chain, visible disclosure,
machine-readable provenance, and counsel or accountable policy decision.
