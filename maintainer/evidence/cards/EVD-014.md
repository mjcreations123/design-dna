# Visual provenance and user control

## Claim

Visual-content users may value origin labeling and control over generated material.

## Observation

Pinterest introduced GenAI labels and controls in visual-inspiration categories in response to user demand for greater control.

## Scope and limitations

This is a platform announcement, not a general scientific or legal rule for every website.

## Counterexamples

Generated imagery can be appropriate when it does not impersonate factual evidence and required disclosure or approval is handled.

## Positive action

Track origin, rights, factual status, transformations, and owner approval for nontrivial assets.

## Supports

`RISK-ASSET-001`

## Validation

Inspect asset manifests and rendered context rather than relying on filenames.
