# Commercial pattern catalog

## Claim

Hero pills, gradient headlines, fake stats, glass, mockups, over-animation, and generic copy are useful perception candidates.

## Observation

The vendor article groups these recurring patterns as signs readers may associate with generated websites.

## Scope and limitations

The source is commercial, subjective, and does not publish a representative method. It cannot support universal rules.

## Counterexamples

Every listed pattern can be useful when its content is real, its role is clear, and implementation is strong.

## Positive action

Use the catalog to seed blinded reviews and code-level candidate scans only.
