# Content design follows user needs and findability

## Claim

Content hierarchy and information architecture should be planned around user needs, grouping, and findability rather than generic section formulas.

## Observation

GOV.UK content guidance treats planning, grouping, and helping users find information as part of content design.

## Scope and limitations

Government service content is not the voice or structure for every brand, campaign, or editorial site.

## Counterexamples

Expressive brand language can coexist with task-oriented information architecture.

## Positive action

Define user needs, message hierarchy, routes, navigation labels, content owners, and plain task-specific actions before polishing prose.
