# Critique should connect observation, effect, principle, and move

## Claim

Design critique becomes actionable when it traces an observed relationship to
its visitor effect, the local project principle at stake, the smallest
corrective move, and a rendered rerun.

## Observation

The pinned MIT VS Code design-philosophy skill demonstrates a values-to-
principles-to-moves chain and names real tensions such as calm versus density
and consistency versus a genuinely special case.

## Scope and limitations

VS Code's tokens, spacing ramp, radii, weights, icon sizes, casing, density, and
product vocabulary belong to VS Code. They cannot govern unrelated sites.

## Counterexamples

A critical warning may deliberately break calm; a campaign may choose visual
dissonance; an editorial project may value discovery over immediate product-
interface legibility.

## Positive action

Begin with the observed relationship, name the visitor consequence, trace it to
this brief or assurance boundary, acknowledge competing values, make the
smallest coherent correction, and rerender the exact condition.

## Supports

`RISK-HIER-001`, `RISK-UX-001`

## Validation

Review records must separate observation from inference and show that the
correction improved the declared principle without importing VS Code's visual
system.

## Retention

Pinned public MIT platform guidance. Review within 90 days and preserve the
project-specificity limit.
