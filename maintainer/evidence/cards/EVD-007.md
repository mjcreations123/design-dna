# Documented builder font defaults

## Claim

One builder's documented starting pool is useful maintainer-only provenance
for studying cross-output convergence. It must not become a runtime selection,
rejection, substitution, or warning rule for an individual project.

## Observation

Pagesmith documents Inter as preloaded on every new site and lists a curated family pool its AI chooses from first.

## Scope and limitations

This is one platform's documentation, not a census of models, builders, countries, or sites. Presence of a listed family says nothing about authorship.

## Counterexamples

Inter and every family in the pool can be correct for brand, reading, language, licensing, or performance reasons.

## Positive action

Use this evidence only when maintainers compare aggregated outputs or refresh
ecosystem research. Runtime typography selection stays open and is decided
from real copy, project fit, rights, language coverage, delivery, and rendered
behavior.

## Supports

`RISK-TYPE-001`

## Validation

Recheck the documented pool quarterly and test aggregate skill-output
repetition without exposing named-family prompts to the producing runtime.

## Retention

Public platform documentation; review within 90 days.
