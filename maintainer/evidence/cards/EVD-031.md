# Rendered alternatives make divergence reviewable

## Claim

For an open design problem, compare rendered alternatives grounded in project
context before propagating one direction through the build.

## Observation

Figma documents functional previews, exploration of multiple solutions,
attachment of more than one design for context, iterative edits, reusable
component and token guidance, and copying preview snapshots into design layers
for comparison and further editing.

## Scope and limitations

This is vendor documentation of available capabilities. It does not establish
that generated alternatives are materially different, high quality, legally
usable, accessible, or appropriate for a particular brief.

## Counterexamples

A bounded change within an approved design system may need one inherited
direction. A rendered comparison is still useful when the brief is open,
expressive, high-stakes, or vulnerable to premature convergence.

## Positive action

Ground exploration in the brief, real content, approved assets, mixed
references, and existing system constraints. Pause long enough to challenge the
first plausible direction, then produce only the amount and form of comparable
evidence needed to expose consequential alternatives. A bounded inherited
change may need one proof; open or high-stakes work may need more. Preserve the
selected checkpoint.

## Supports

`RISK-EXPLORE-001`, `RISK-SYS-001`

## Validation

Confirm the project record states which default was challenged, what
alternatives or counterevidence were considered, how consequential decisions
differed, which criteria governed the choice, and why the selected direction
won. Do not require a universal hypothesis or proof count; compare rendered
slices when that evidence is useful for the decision.

## Retention

Public platform documentation; retain a dated summary and review within 90
days because product capabilities can change.
