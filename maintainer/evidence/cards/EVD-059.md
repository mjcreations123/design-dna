# A visually safe photo can still disclose its capture location

## Claim

Planned-public image and video review must inspect delivered-file metadata as
well as visible content.

## Observation

Apple documents that cameras can embed location coordinates into photos and
videos and that people receiving those files may be able to access the location
metadata.

## Scope and limitations

The guide addresses Apple photo workflows and location specifically. Other
formats and tools may carry different metadata, thumbnails, authorship,
provenance, rights, or device fields.

## Counterexamples

Intentional public location metadata may serve a documentary or mapping task.
Removing every metadata field can also destroy needed orientation, color,
rights, attribution, or validated provenance information.

## Positive action

Inspect visible and embedded data, decide what is necessary, remove sensitive
location and device information where applicable, then re-open and re-inspect
the exact public derivative.

## Supports

`RISK-ASSET-001`

## Validation

The public derivative has a recorded metadata inspection, safe public filename,
visual/orientation/color recheck, exact hash, and a restricted-original
boundary.

## Retention

Public official safety guidance. Review within 90 days and preserve the
asset-specific decision rather than a blind strip-everything rule.
