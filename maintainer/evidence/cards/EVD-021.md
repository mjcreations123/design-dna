# Owner default for arbitrary prominent-copy fragments

## Claim

This installation should avoid coloring or restyling an arbitrary final word or short fragment in prominent display copy merely to manufacture personality.

## Observation

The owner explicitly requested this behavior after identifying it as an unwanted recurring pattern in generated site designs.

## Scope and limitations

This is a personal house preference, not research proving authorship or universal poor design.

## Counterexamples

Approved brand lockups, complete semantic phrases, links, status, warnings, data categories, quotations, editorial conventions, and product syntax may require distinct treatment.

## Positive action

Use stronger copy, grammatical line breaks, overall hierarchy, or a complete meaning-bearing treatment first; preserve accessible project-specific exceptions.

`RISK-EMPH-001`

## Validation

Test both the default behavior and explicit brand/semantic exceptions in Claude and Codex.
