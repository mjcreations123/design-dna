# Image purpose determines treatment

## Claim

Image alternatives and presentation should follow the image's actual informational, functional, or decorative role.

## Observation

The W3C tutorial distinguishes informative, decorative, functional, text-containing, complex, group, and image-map cases.

## Scope and limitations

The tutorial does not prescribe art direction, licensing, or performance strategy.

## Counterexamples

The same visual may require different treatment in a different context because its role changed.

## Positive action

Record image role, responsive crop, caption, alternative, provenance, and performance behavior together.

## Supports

`RISK-ASSET-001`

## Validation

Review images in rendered context and with assistive technology where relevant.
