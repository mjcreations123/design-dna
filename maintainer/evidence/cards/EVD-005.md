# Semantic color cannot rely on color alone

## Claim

Meaning-bearing text emphasis must not use color as its only cue.

## Observation

WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 1.4.1 requires another visual or programmatic means when color conveys information, action, response, or distinction.

## Scope and limitations

The criterion does not prohibit decorative color or require all words in a heading to share one color.

## Counterexamples

Links, statuses, data categories, warnings, offers, and brand treatments may use color when meaning remains available through another cue.

## Positive action

Keep headings coherent by default and make semantic exceptions accessible and system-defined.

## Supports

`RISK-UX-001`

## Validation

Test contrast, non-color cues, semantic structure, localization, and responsive wrapping.
