# Vibe-coding lifecycle, homogenization, and productive friction

## Claim

Unexamined defaults can enter at every stage of a vibe-coded website, and deliberate contextual friction can reduce homogenization.

## Observation

The paper maps prompting, code generation, rendering, feedback, and deployment; it describes global defaults overriding regional preference, unmodified framework defaults eroding brand identity, and contextual anchoring plus active elicitation as mitigations.

## Scope and limitations

This is a sociotechnical risk analysis and case-study framework, not an authorship detector or prevalence estimate for any one visual motif.

## Counterexamples

Familiar frameworks and global systems can be correct when selected deliberately and adapted to real task, brand, and regional needs.

## Positive action

Inspect existing context, surface assumptions, compare alternatives, render, solicit feedback, and revise defaults before release.
