# Separate structure from visual concept selection

## Claim

Information architecture, wireframe structure, and visual concept selection are
distinct decisions that should be made and reviewed in a deliberate sequence.

## Observation

Relume documents creating visual concepts only for projects that already have a
sitemap and wireframes, creating multiple concepts, defining reusable color,
typography, and UI settings, and presenting up to three concepts with
descriptions for stakeholder review.

## Scope and limitations

This is a vendor workflow built around Relume components. It does not prove
that its generated concepts are materially divergent, suitable for every
project, or better than hand-directed exploration.

## Counterexamples

A small route inside a settled system can inherit its information architecture
and visual language. A new site, major redesign, or unresolved brief benefits
from separating structural reasoning from concept selection.

## Positive action

Resolve audiences, priority tasks, route relationships, and core content before
visual styling. Then compare concepts as coherent systems with named intent and
consequences, rather than treating palette, typeface, or decoration swaps as
different directions.

## Supports

`RISK-EXPLORE-001`, `RISK-IA-001`

## Validation

Check that the sitemap and representative wireframe precede visual
propagation, and that concept review records meaningful differences in
hierarchy, composition, interaction, media, pacing, and system behavior.

Public platform documentation; retain a dated summary and review within 90
days because product workflows can change.
