# Geist as a v0 ecosystem default candidate

## Claim

The reported ecosystem connection is a low-confidence, maintainer-only lead
for provenance and aggregate convergence research. It must never become an
individual project's selection, rejection, substitution, or warning rule.

## Observation

A Vercel community support reply says Geist can enter projects through v0's default design system; Vercel templates and v0 examples also commonly use the family.

## Scope and limitations

This is a support-thread observation, not stable product documentation or a representative output census. v0 behavior can change.

## Counterexamples

Geist is a capable, intentional Vercel brand family and can be correct for developer tools, existing Vercel systems, and readable product interfaces.

## Positive action

Keep the lead in maintainer research only. Runtime typography selection stays
open and project-led, with no additional scrutiny triggered by the family
name. Preserve legitimate brand or ecosystem continuity when it serves the
work.

## Validation

Test aggregated, fresh v0-related outputs at the maintainer layer and retire
this candidate if the reported behavior no longer appears. Do not surface the
name to the producing runtime.
