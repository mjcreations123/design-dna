# Design reasoning is a claim to verify, not evidence by itself

## Claim

Concrete design and behavior commitments need direct reconciliation against the
delivered interface; fluent rationale cannot stand in for implementation.

## Observation

The 2026 Design Theater study evaluated 120 interfaces from five generative UI
tools and found material reasoning-to-artifact gaps, weak implementation of
implicit functional principles, and convergence that varied more in color than
in appearance or layout.

## Scope and limitations

This is one recent preprint with a bounded task set and tool sample. Its metrics
do not define beauty, authorship, cultural fit, or universal design quality.

## Counterexamples

A concise implementation may correctly omit a rejected idea when the record is
updated. Vague aspiration is not a testable commitment and should not be scored
as though it were one.

## Positive action

Extract only concrete, verifiable commitments; compare each with code, rendered
states, and behavior; mark it realized, partial, missing, or superseded; and
reopen consequential missing decisions before making completion claims.
