# Web-layout homogenization predates current generators

## Claim

Visual similarity on the web is not unique to generative AI, so familiar layout cannot establish authorship.

## Observation

The study reports increasing structural similarity among representative websites and discusses common libraries, mobile constraints, and design trends as contributors.

## Scope and limitations

The study predates current vibe-coding platforms and does not judge the quality or authorship of individual sites.

## Counterexamples

Conventional structure can be the clearest and most accessible solution for a real task.

## Positive action

Derive structure from information and user decisions; seek specificity through content and system craft rather than novelty alone.
