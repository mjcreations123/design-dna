# Dark patterns impair informed choice

## Claim

Design that disguises advertising, hides terms or fees, obstructs cancellation, or manipulates data consent can subvert user choice.

## Observation

The FTC staff report documents deceptive and unfair interface patterns across commerce, subscriptions, privacy, and advertising.

## Scope and limitations

Legal application depends on jurisdiction and facts; Design DNA is not legal advice.

## Counterexamples

Accurate urgency, risk warnings, and consequential confirmations can be prominent when proportionate and truthful.

## Positive action

Make price, consequence, consent, alternative, cancellation, and exit paths clear and comparably usable.

## Supports

`RISK-DARK-001`

## Validation

Review persuasion and checkout fixtures for hidden cost, urgency, consent, and cancellation behavior.
