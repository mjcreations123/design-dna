# Voice stays coherent while tone follows the situation

## Claim

A site needs a recognizable project-specific voice, while its tone should
adapt to the user, task, emotional state, and moment in the journey.

## Observation

Atlassian distinguishes stable personality traits from tone that changes with
the user's situation. Its guidance varies expression by audience and emotional
context while preserving recognizable vocabulary and experience across
interfaces, websites, and communications.

## Scope and limitations

Atlassian's named traits and examples belong to Atlassian. They are evidence
for a voice-and-tone method, not a house voice to copy into unrelated brands.

## Counterexamples

Regulated, emergency, technical, luxury, or deliberately neutral contexts can
require narrower tonal movement. Consistency still applies to terminology,
clarity, and the approved character of the service.

## Positive action

Derive a small set of observable voice traits from approved brand material and
real audience needs. Define how those traits change across orientation,
decision, error, waiting, success, and support moments, then apply one
terminology ledger across the experience.

## Supports

`RISK-COPY-001`

## Validation

Run a cross-page voice pass and a state-by-state tone pass. Check that a reader
can recognize the same organization without every message using the same
cadence, joke, adjective, or promotional intensity.
