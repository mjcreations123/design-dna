# A shared identity can vary through several coordinated channels

## Claim

Coherence can come from a common conceptual origin expressed through form,
color, motion, sound, and composition rather than from repeating one page
layout.

## Observation

NRK's public identity guidance describes a common philosophy behind multiple
identity markers and a flexible meeting-point concept that changes across
design, animation, and interaction. It pairs distinctiveness and liveliness
with empathy and broad access.

## Scope and limitations

This is NRK's proprietary brand guidance, written in Norwegian, and reflects
its public-broadcasting context. Its logo-derived forms, language, principles,
and exact expression must not be copied into unrelated work.

## Counterexamples

Applying the same logo shape everywhere can become a repetitive surface recipe.
A project without sound or motion should not add them merely to occupy every
possible channel.

## Positive action

Derive a project-owned identity premise, choose only the channels that clarify
it, and vary their expression by route purpose while preserving access and
recognition.

## Supports

`RISK-SYS-001`, `RISK-REPEAT-001`

## Validation

Remove each dominant channel in turn and ask whether the identity and route
job remain legible. Compare all routes for a shared origin, purposeful
variation, and over-propagated motifs.

## Retention

Public first-party design guidance; retain a dated translated summary and
review within 90 days. Do not reuse NRK-owned visual assets or claim NRK
endorsement.
