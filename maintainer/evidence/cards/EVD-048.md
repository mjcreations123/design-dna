# Interface review needs mechanisms, not wholesale style defaults

## Claim

Stable semantic, keyboard, focus, form, motion, content-resilience, image, and
state checks are useful review inputs when selected by the actual surface.

## Observation

The pinned MIT-licensed Vercel corpus covers labels, semantics, keyboard and
focus behavior, forms, reduced motion, robust content, image dimensions, safe
areas, locale formatting, destructive actions, and interaction states.

## Scope and limitations

Its Title Case preference, APCA recommendation, URL-state expectations, and
latency guidance are contextual. They cannot replace project voice, normative
WCAG conformance, platform contracts, or measured requirements. The separate
agent wrapper's mutable remote-fetch pattern is not adopted.

## Counterexamples

A static publication may not need URL-backed UI state; an approved product
system may use sentence case; a task can require latency or interaction behavior
different from a portable threshold.

## Positive action

Retain only mechanism-level checks whose project conditions apply. Resolve
conflicts in favor of truth, the declared product contract, current standards,
and the project's voice and audience.

## Supports

`RISK-UX-001`, `RISK-RESP-001`, `RISK-CODE-001`

## Validation

Exercise the applicable behavior in a real browser and record intentional
not-applicable cases. Confirm that no casing, URL, latency, or contrast method
was promoted into a universal design rule.

## Retention

Pinned public MIT-licensed platform guidance with a hash-bound summary. Review
within 90 days because the corpus and browser recommendations can change.
