# Type roles and meaning-bearing emphasis

## Claim

Color and italic type are strongest when they correspond to an established semantic role.

## Observation

Carbon uses neutral running-text roles and reserves colored or italic type for defined purposes such as links, actions, code, warnings, alerts, and emphasis.

## Scope and limitations

Carbon is one design system, not a universal aesthetic standard or AI detector.

## Counterexamples

Editorial and brand systems may define broader expressive type roles.

## Positive action

Define emphasis roles at the system level and default prominent headings to a coherent treatment.
