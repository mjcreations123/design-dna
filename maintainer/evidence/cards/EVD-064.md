# Public regression cases and protected promotion cases serve different jobs

## Claim

Qualification needs a protected, refreshed partition in addition to public
development and immutable regression cases, or repeated tuning can turn the
suite into rehearsal evidence.

## Observation

LiveBench addresses contamination by refreshing questions and grounding them
in recent material, while favoring verifiable ground truth rather than an LLM
judge for its objective domains.

## Scope and limitations

Open-ended design cannot use objective answers for every judgment. Protected
holdouts reduce leakage but do not replace independent perception, task,
accessibility, cultural, factual, or owner review.

## Counterexamples

Public regression cases are valuable because maintainers can reproduce exact
failures. They should remain public and immutable rather than being mislabeled
as secret promotion evidence.

## Positive action

Separate `dev`, immutable `regression`, and external
`promotion-holdout` partitions; keep holdout prompts and expected judgments out
of the shipped package; rotate them after exposure; record partition coverage.

## Supports

`RISK-REVIEW-001`

## Validation

A promotion claim fails when holdout evidence is absent, stored inside the
public repository, reused after disclosure, or merged with development results
without a partition record.
