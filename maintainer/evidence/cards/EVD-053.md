# Detect the relationship, not a universal aesthetic verdict

## Claim

Static candidate detection can reliably surface an exact pre-heading or
repeated-label relationship for review without deciding that the visible label
is automatically defective or AI-authored.

## Observation

The pinned Apache-2.0 Impeccable repository includes executable detectors and
fixtures for hero eyebrows, kickers, numbered section labels, and repeated
container text. That mechanism directly addresses the owner's recurring
"The optical study" failure.

## Scope and limitations

Impeccable also labels many fonts, palettes, motion choices, and kickers as
universal AI tells. Those verdicts are not transferable evidence. A source
scanner cannot know whether a category, source, date, sequence, status,
taxonomy, or editorial convention is visitor-useful.

## Counterexamples

An article category, archive source, date, real progress step, filter, tab,
legend, or brand editorial device can belong immediately above a heading.

## Positive action

Report the exact path, line, label text, nearby heading, and relationship as a
non-gating candidate. Apply semantic exemptions, require contextual rendered
review, and never infer authorship.

## Supports

`RISK-LABEL-001`, `RISK-COPYFORM-001`

## Validation

Fixtures must catch construction-facing heading-adjacent labels and repeated
numbered scaffolding while leaving real categories, dates, status, progress,
filters, tabs, breadcrumbs, and legends unflagged.

## Retention

Pinned public Apache-2.0 practitioner implementation. Review within 90 days and
retain the non-gating, contextual-verdict limit.
