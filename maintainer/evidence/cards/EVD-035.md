# Complex forms require a humane recoverable journey

## Claim

A complex form is a service journey, not a stack of controls; it should manage
expectations, cognitive load, progress, assistance, interruption, and recovery.

## Observation

USWDS guidance calls for transparent data expectations, mobile and
constrained-bandwidth design, progressive disclosure, meaningful question
chunks, context-appropriate progress cues, save-and-resume, access to help,
calm actionable validation, and recovery without blame.

## Scope and limitations

This federal service pattern is not a universal form recipe. Linear step
indicators, unrestricted section order, or save-and-resume may be inappropriate
for short, conditional, anonymous, transactional, or security-sensitive flows.

## Counterexamples

A short single-purpose form can remain on one page. A nonlinear eligibility
flow may need a progress model based on completed tasks rather than a fixed
step count.

## Positive action

Map the complete service journey and its interruption points. Group questions
by user task, explain why sensitive information is needed, design for mobile
and constrained conditions, preserve valid work, provide contextual help, and
make errors specific, calm, and recoverable.

## Supports

`RISK-UX-001`, `RISK-IA-001`

## Validation

Test representative happy, error, interruption, resume, changed-answer, help,
mobile, keyboard, assistive-technology, and constrained-network journeys with
realistic content and applicable privacy and security requirements.
