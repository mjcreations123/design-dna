# Accessibility quality baseline

## Claim

Accessible semantics, keyboard behavior, focus, contrast, reflow, input, and motion handling are part of production-quality web design.

## Observation

WCAG 2.2 defines testable success criteria across perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust experiences.

## Scope and limitations

Applicable conformance, jurisdiction, technology, and exceptions must be established per project. Compliance does not guarantee good art direction or task usability.

## Counterexamples

Expressive design and motion remain possible when equivalent access and preference behavior are preserved.

## Positive action

Default public work to a documented WCAG 2.2 AA target unless project or legal requirements supersede it, then record tested and untested criteria.

## Supports

`RISK-UX-001`, `RISK-RESP-001`

## Validation

Combine automated candidates with manual keyboard, zoom, screen-reader, contrast, reflow, and task checks.

## Retention

Public W3C standard; review for errata and successor standards.
