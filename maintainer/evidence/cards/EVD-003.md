# Web-scale counterevidence to visual absolutes

## Claim

Purple, gradients, shadows, and particular fonts are not reliable standalone indicators of generated authorship.

## Observation

The HTTP Archive analysis found no statistically significant surge in gradients, shadows, or specific fonts and suggested that some color growth could reflect Tailwind adoption.

## Scope and limitations

Implementation census data does not measure every visitor's perception or the quality of a particular art direction.

## Counterexamples

Any named color, font, gradient, or shadow can be intentional, accessible, and project-specific.

## Positive action

Review clusters, rationale, implementation, and project fit instead of inferring authorship from one token.

## Supports

`RISK-NOV-001`, `RISK-VIS-001`, `RISK-TYPE-001`

## Validation

Require the risk rubric and scanner to avoid single-token pass/fail rules.
