# Perceived signals do not establish the design process

## Claim

Readers can disagree about AI-like appearance and can attribute model authorship to work whose layout and font choices were made by a person.

## Observation

In the thread, commenters disagree about the same design; the poster says the layout was designed in Figma and the font was personally selected, while some readers still cite the font and imagery as AI-like.

## Scope and limitations

The poster's account is not independently verified, and one thread cannot estimate false-positive rates.

## Counterexamples

Strong generated signals can still identify real quality problems such as malformed imagery, weak hierarchy, or overdone effects even when they do not identify authorship.

## Positive action

Describe observable quality, context, and implementation issues; never convert a motif or perception score into an authorship claim.

## Supports

`RISK-NOV-001`, `RISK-TYPE-001`, `RISK-ASSET-001`

## Validation

Blind reviewers to tool and process labels, record disagreement, and score quality dimensions instead of guessing authorship.
